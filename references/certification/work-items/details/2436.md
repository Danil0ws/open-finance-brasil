Hello @danilodelgado. Thanks for raising this.

According to the Enrollments v2.2.0 specification:

> data.rp = Identificador único da Relying Party, que corresponde ao valor do CN do certificado de transporte da iniciadora.

Regarding the best approach to validate this field, our recommendation is:

1. **Extract the client certificate used in the mTLS connection** for the POST /fido-registration-options call.
2. **Read the CN from that certificate** (this represents the initiator’s transport identity, and therefore its Relying Party identifier).
3. **Compare the rp value from the JSON payload with that CN**:
   * If rp matches the CN, proceed with the FIDO flow.
   * If rp does **not** match, return HTTP 422 with RP_INVALIDA and reject the enrollment due to FIDO failure (REJEITADO_FALHA_FIDO).

In the step FAPIBrazilExtractClientMTLSCertificateSubject, you can observe an example of CS behavior:

![image.png](/uploads/5e994b1527db79a196a6a2ebe671cfc0/image.png){width=900 height=138}

Please let us know if you need any additional clarification on this validation.
