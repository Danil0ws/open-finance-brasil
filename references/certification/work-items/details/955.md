## 📝 Descrição

## 1. What happened and what were you expecting?

The `payments_api_multiple-consents-canc-iniciadora-conditional_test-module_v3` seems to be using, incorrectly, a v2 validator in which there were no `authorisationFlow` field. However, according to [API Payment Initiation 3.0.0-rc.1](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/OpenBanking-Brasil/openapi/main/swagger-apis/payments/3.0.0-rc.1.yml), this is a optional field that should be expected.
