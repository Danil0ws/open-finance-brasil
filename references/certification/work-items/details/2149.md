Thanks for contacting us.

The DETALHE_EDICAO_INVALIDO error is expected in this case because the PATCH request you made did not comply with the business rules outlined for consent edition. According to the test scenario, the riskSignals property is mandatory for the consent edition process, and its omission triggers the DETALHE_EDICAO_INVALIDO error.

While FALTAM_SINAIS_OBRIGATORIOS_PLATAFORMA refers to missing required signals from the platform side, it specifically applies when the riskSignals or other required platform-specific fields are entirely missing or incomplete from the request.

In this case, since the riskSignals property was explicitly expected by the test for the PATCH request, it triggers DETALHE_EDICAO_INVALIDO, indicating that the request failed due to business rules not being respected (such as missing required data for consent edition).

If you do not agree with this interpretation, we suggest opening a ticket for Chicago to evaluate the situation further.

Please let us know if further clarification is needed!
