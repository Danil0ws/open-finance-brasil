## 📝 Descrição

## 1. What happened and what were you expecting?

The test module expects a 422 UNPROCESSABLE_ENTITY as the response for the "schedule.single.date field defined as yesterday D+370" validation.

As described in version 4.0.0-beta.3 of the specification:
"The maximum term for the consent must be two years, counting from the date of creation of the consent returned in the creation of the consent (field /data/creationDateTime)."

In regards to schedule.single.date field:
"This field must always be at least D+1 calendar days, i.e. the date immediately following the date of consent, taking into account the Brasilia time zone, and must be at most D+730 calendar days from the date of consent, also taking into account the Brasilia time zone."

For that reason the test module must be updated so that is compliant with the new date upper limit stablished in beta.3.
