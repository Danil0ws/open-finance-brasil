We understand that you've noticed the test fails when the payment status transitions to `PDNG`, and that, according to the specification, `PDNG` is a valid intermediate status for Pix payments, occurring before `ACCP`.

However, the Conformance Suit's expectation regarding the `PDNG` status in sandbox environments is based on the intended flow for this specific test, which aims to ensure consent edition can be applied to make more restrictive limits. The logic behind this validation is as follows:

* **Standard Payment Flow for Test Scenarios:** In a certification test scenario, especially one designed to test expected payment outcomes, payments are expected to follow a "standard successful path." This means that after being received (`RCVD`), they should ideally transition to `ACCP` (Pix ready) or `SCHD` (Pix scheduled), depending on the payment type and scheduling.
* **`PDNG` (Pending) Status:** The `PDNG` status indicates that the Pix transaction has been temporarily held for analysis. In sandbox tests, **payments are generally not expected to enter an analysis state**. Sandbox environments are designed to simulate ideal flows or controlled failures, not to emulate analysis holds that typically occur in production environments for security or risk reasons. This test's objective is to validate specific transitions for consent edition and restrictive limits, where an intermediate `PDNG` state is not part of the expected "successful" or "rejected by restriction" paths that the test is designed to verify.

Therefore, the non-acceptance of the `PDNG` status in this specific certification test occurs because it is not expected for test transactions in a sandbox environment to enter an analysis state (`PDNG`) as part of the standard successful or restricted flows this test validates. Besides that, this test is designed to drive the payment to specific expected states (`SCHD` or `RJCT` for restrictive limits) to validate the consent edition and subsequent payment behavior, which would be bypassed or delayed by a `PDNG` status.
