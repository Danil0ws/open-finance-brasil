## 📝 Descrição

## 1. What Happened and what were you expecting?

Tests that contain `OpfPaginationValidator` (like `financings_api_resources_test-module_v2` and `invoice-financings_api_core_test-module_v2`) do not identify the page number in links when there are multiple pages. It follows below the error thrown:

![image](/uploads/687f2caf4d0bcaaab8c02c370a988c2d/image.png)

It is worth mentioning: self link does not requires `page` parameter as it is 1 by default and the links do not have `page-size` parameter because they are 25 by default, which is the value adopted.
