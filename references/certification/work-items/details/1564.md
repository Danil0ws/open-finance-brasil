Hi Esdras! Thanks for the ticket!

Although the fields riskSignals are required at the specification, this is a business Validation that should not be executed in this case, since the token is not valid for this endpoint.

In that sense, although this is true, even without the riskSignals you should be returning a 403, since security validations should be executed prior to the business validations.
