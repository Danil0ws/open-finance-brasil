Hi @gimendes 

I understand your point about the use of `client_credentials` , your reading of the documentation is correct regarding the query endpoints. However, this test has a specific objective.

The test `automatic-payments_api_recurring-payments-extraday-retry-cancel_test-module` was created to validate the **payment retry** flow without the need for new user interaction. As you can see below:

![image.png](/uploads/1ff82bb4c5631ae0ca87876b091fec26/image.png)

The quote you provided from the specification - that the `client_credentials` token is an option for **query** (`GET`) endpoints - does not apply to this test, as the call in question is a **`POST`** for a new payment attempt.

The intention of the test is to verify whether your application supports the functionality of performing a new payment attempt (`retry`) automatically, using a `client_credentials` token.

In short, your application needs to be prepared to accept `POST` requests at the retry endpoint using a `client_credentials` token, as this is the behavior the conformance suit seeks to validate. 

Please, feel free to reach out in case of further questions!
