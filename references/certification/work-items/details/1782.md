Hi Mauricio,

The bilateral agreements are being deprecated in version 2.0.0, so they are no longer required. We understand that they are still included in the v2.00 test plan, but they will be removed from there. In the previous version, v1.3.0, there wasn't a clear definition of how the bilateral agreements should be implemented. The conformance suite now requires a clientID that does not have a bilateral agreement, and this is being used in the test module.

We are happy to assist with any additional needs!
