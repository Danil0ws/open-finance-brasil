## 1. What Happened and what were you expecting?
There is a wrong validation happening in the "warrantySubType" field. Our API is returning the value "EQUIPAMENTOS", but the test failed expecting the value "EQUIPAMENTOS FATURA_CARTAO_CREDITO", and it's wrong. These are 2 different types.

The `ENUM_WARRANTY_SUB_TYPE` ENUM at `FinancingGuaranteesResponseValidator` is wrong.
