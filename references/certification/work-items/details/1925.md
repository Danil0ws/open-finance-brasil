## 📝 Descrição

**Description:**\
In the Automatic Pix Beta.2 version, the `startDateTime` field was moved from the root data to inside each product under the `recurringConfiguration` field. For the **sweeping product**, a new restriction was introduced:

> **\[Restrição\]** Caso esse campo não seja enviado pelo iniciador na requisição, o detentor deve preencher esse campo com o mesmo valor atribuído ao campo `/data/creationDateTime`.

However, despite this restriction, the `startDateTime` field was made **mandatory**, which prevents the initiator from omitting the field and relying on the account holder to replicate the value using `creationDateTime`:

![image.png](/uploads/e055af849836678512b4d1dabf90f082/image.png){width=542 height=455}

**Proposed Solution:**\
To align with the restriction and allow the account holder to set the `startDateTime` field when it is not provided by the initiator, the field should be changed to **optional** in the schema, otherwise the account holder will respond with a 400 message.

This adjustment will enable the initiator to omit the field while ensuring compliance with the requirement to replicate the value from `creationDateTime`.

**In that sense, what we would like confirmation from the wqorking group:**\
Should the startDateTime be optional at the POST Recurring Consents request?
