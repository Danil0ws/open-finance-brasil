Hi,

Thanks for opening the ticket!

The mentioned attached images could not be found on the issue description.

Nonetheless, the test module failed due to the requestDateTime provided in the response_body for request GET /operations.

As described in the [API Exchanges specification](https://openfinancebrasil.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/OF/pages/345113312/Informa+es+T+cnicas+-+DC+C+mbio+-+v1.0.0) Get /operations endpoint:

> meta.requestDateTime: Data e hora da consulta, conforme especificação RFC-3339, formato UTC.

While the requestDateTime being provided is in timezone UTC-3, thus not being compliant to what is determined by the spec.

Therefore conformance suit is working as expected in this regard, please make sure that requestDateTime field is sent on the expected timezone.

Feel free to open a new ticket or reopen this one if you have any other questions,
