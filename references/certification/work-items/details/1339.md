## 📝 Descrição

## 1\. What happened and what were you expecting?

The payments_api_multiple-consents-conditional_test-module_v4 test is validating the expirationDateTime field, and in my case it's complaining that the dateTime is incorrect for the PARTIALLY_ACCEPTED status.

What is this expirationDateTime supposed to be? The specification does not mention a specific time limit for the PARTIALLY_ACCEPTED status. [Máquina de Estados - v4.0.0-rc.1 - \[SV\] Pagamentos - Área do Desenvolvedor - Open Finance Brasil - Área do Desenvolvedor (atlassian.net)](https://openfinancebrasil.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/OF/pages/264306933/M+quina+de+Estados+-+v4.0.0-rc.1+-+SV+Pagamentos)

I tried using 10 minutes after the consent creation, 10 minutes after the PARTIALLY_ACCEPTED status and the next day, but none passed. What dateTime should I use? 5 minutes like the AWAITING_AUTHORISATION status? That seems too low for all multiple users to authorize the consent.

Please advise or list in the test log what the expirationDateTime is supposed to be.

Hi Francielle! Thanks fot the ticket!

The way the conformance suite is validating this is that we set that the expirationDateTime should not be more than 5 minutes after the creationDateTime, in the same way this is done with awaiting_authorization

On the swagger, it is mentioned that:

 **O consentimento é criado com o status AWAITING_AUTHORISATION, e deve assumir o status AUTHORIZED ou REJECTED antes do tempo de expiração - 5 minutos. Caso o tempo seja expirado, o status deve assumir REJECTED**.

Because its unclear in the swagger, we will raise this question to the WG and update here once we have a response back.
