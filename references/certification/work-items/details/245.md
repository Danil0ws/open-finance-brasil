## 1. What Happened and what were you expecting?

There is a wrong validation happening in the "procurators.type" field. The spec on ENUM EnumProcuratorsTypePersonal has the values REPRESENTANTE_LEGAL, PROCURADOR and NAO_APLICA, but the test at PersonalRelationsResponseValidator validates the value NAO_SE_APLICA
