Hi @lucas.barros7. Thanks for raising this.

In your config there's a key at the top of resource called transactionIdentification. The test actually looks for **transactionIdentifier** in that spot, which is the name every payments config uses. So just rename that top-level key from transactionIdentification to transactionIdentifier and keep the same value. That clears the "Transaction Identifier field was null" error.

Only that top-level key. The transactionIdentification inside the QRDN consents (brazilQrdnPaymentConsent1/2) is correct, it's the real field in the payment, so leave it as it is.

The form isn't showing the transactionIdentifier field for this test right now, that's why you couldn't find it. A fix for that is on the way, so soon you won't need to edit the JSON by hand.

We'll keep you posted here.
