## 📝 Descrição

Hello Guys,

I'm a software developer at BS2 Bank, we have been trying to get done the test payments-api-qres-good-email-proxy-test, but every time our transactions are been dinied by Brazil Central Bank. (The ISPB contained in the payment order identifier is different from the ISPB of the debtor or payer).

We have already contacted the Brazilian Bank and they replied us that the validation is right.

The problem we have noticed is the test is not following the test plan configuration, i have changed the QrCode, initiator's Document, EndToEndId, but even that way our payments from OpenBanking with the same ISPB into the EndToEndId and also the same QrCode.

We are olny having this trouble in the QRCode's tests, the others are still working successfully.

The Hypothesis brought by the group was that something is not being done correctly on the request that is being sent to the SPI. Unfortunately, we cannot help evaluate this request as it's out of the Conformance Suite Engineering Team Scope.

@erick.domingues1 The problem is the creditor's account and the QRCode used on the test are not from the initiator's ISPB.
Even changing the cnpjInitiator property we are still receiving the EndToEndId with Brazil Bank ISPB: E**00000000**. Look to the file "Test_Pan_V2.text"

That's why the brazil central bank started to reject our open banking's transactions, since 13-10-2022 they have implemented that validation comparing the ISPB from EndToEndId and the ISPB from the creditor and debitor accounts, before that validation have been implemented we were passing that test.

What i'm saying is that the QrCode should be from the initiator bank and have his ISPB, so that way the creditor's account also would be from the initiator bank and that way the test should be done successfully. Or the EndToEndId's ISPB should be from the QrCode bank ISPB, like: E**99999004**202211060833XXXXX
