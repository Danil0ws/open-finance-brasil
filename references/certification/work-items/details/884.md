## 📝 Descrição

## 1. What happened and what were you expecting?

The latest stable release of Phase 4B investment APIs introduced changes to two date filter parameters for Funds/Transactions and Fund/Transactions-current endpoints:

- "toTransactionDate" was updated to "toTransactionConversionDate"
- "fromTransactionDate" was updated to "fromTransactionConversionDate"

Consequently this ended up generating errors again when validating self-links, as seen below:

![image](/uploads/f7a22798e968f0194752aa60a6a1f5e0/image.png)

It appears that the `OpfPaginationValidator` is not taking into account the new parameter names during self link validation.
