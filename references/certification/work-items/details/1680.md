## 📝 Descrição

#### Current Behavior:

---

As described at https://gitlab.com/raidiam-conformance/open-finance/certification/-/issues/1651 and discussed at https://gitlab.com/raidiam-conformance/open-finance/certification/-/issues/1659, the conformance suite is currently falling JWT refresh tokens at Consent v3 tests.

#### Expected Behavior:

---

In the new security profile, it is stated that the Authorization Server:

1. _shall issue refresh_tokens (JWT or opaque) without an expiration period in scenarios where it is necessary._

In that sense, the AS could issue refresh tokens JWT without and expiration time in case it is necessary. Since all Phase 2 consents can be extended to indefinite term, we have to change these tests to accept:

* Opaque Refresh Tokens
* JWT refresh tokens, as long as they do not have the "exp" claim
* JWT refresh tokens, as long as the "exp" claim has a value equal or superior to 01-01-2300
