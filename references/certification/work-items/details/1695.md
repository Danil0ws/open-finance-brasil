Hello, Caio!

Thank you for your support on this matter. The documentation regarding the automatic-payment webhook, is that the step that is OPTIONAL is when the consent status get to AUTHORISED from "AWAITING_AUTHORISATION". When the consent status goes from "PARTIALLY_ACCEPTED" to "AUTHORISED" then you must notify.

Here's the documentation for clarification.

"Conditionalities for triggering events
AUTHORISED:

If the consent has reached the AUTHORISED status after having passed through the PARTIALLY_ACCEPTED status, the bank MUST notify the Initiator via Webhook.

If the consent has been transferred directly to AUTHORISED, notification is optional for the bank."
