## 📝 Descrição

#### Current Behavior:
---
The POST recurring-consents incorrectly sends the field `startDateTime` duplicated:
  - `data.startDateTime`
  - `data.recurringConfiguration.sweeping.startDateTime`

The field `data.startDateTime` is incorrectly sent, as shown below:

![image](/uploads/204ae294ad308eafe9d314f35957151d/image.png){width=400}

#### Expected Behavior:
---
The field `data.startDateTime` on POST recurring-consents should have been removed in the transition from v2.0.0-beta.1 to v2.0.0-beta.2, like shown in the specification:
![image](/uploads/9dfecfacb352a9ec2b9f18fae2ba73d4/image.png){width=900}
