## 📝 Descrição

**Observed behavior**

When FVP submits a v4 PIX payment (POST /open-banking/payments/v4/pix/payments) for a consent that uses Schedule.Daily, Weekly, Monthly, or Custom, the PCM additionalInfo carries paymentType=SCHEDULED. PCM ticket 202915 reported two paymentIds created from a Schedule.Daily consent and reported as SCHEDULED by FVP, while the holder reported RECURRENT.

**Expected behavior**

Per [Iniciacao de Pagamentos additionalInfo](https://openfinancebrasil.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/OF/pages/1621622796/Inicia+o+de+Pagamentos+-+Regras+de+Obrigatoriedade+additionalInfo "https://openfinancebrasil.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/OF/pages/1621622796/Inicia+o+de+Pagamentos+-+Regras+de+Obrigatoriedade+additionalInfo") and [API Estados de Pagamento SV](https://openfinancebrasil.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/OF/pages/1211858945/API+Estados+de+Pagamento+-+SV "https://openfinancebrasil.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/OF/pages/1211858945/API+Estados+de+Pagamento+-+SV"), paymentType=RECURRENT must be emitted for payments derived from consents using Schedule.Daily, Weekly, Monthly, or Custom. paymentType=SCHEDULED is reserved for Schedule.Single.
