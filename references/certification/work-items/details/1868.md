#### Current Behavior:
---
The "issuerInstitutionName" field is being validated in a way that makes it impossible to use names such as "Banco do Brasil S A" or even "Organização A1" as the documentation uses in its example. This was first noticed in this issue: [issues/1796](https://gitlab.com/raidiam-conformance/open-finance/certification/-/issues/1796).

#### Expected Behavior:
---
The certification engine is expected to accept the name as well as the documentation. Or that the documentation changes the regex to, for example, [\w\W\s]* as it is used elsewhere.
