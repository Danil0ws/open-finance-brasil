Thanks for the ticket!

The test is correct in creating a consent with `data.recurringConfiguration.automatic.fixedAmount` as `0.50` and expect a payment of `1.00` to succeed because the payment in matter is the first one. This behavior aligns with the specification ([API Automatic payments v2.0.0-beta.2](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/OpenBanking-Brasil/openapi/main/swagger-apis/automatic-payments/2.0.0-beta.2.yml)), as the first payment should be independent from the other ones and must follow the "rules" defined in `data.recurringConfiguration.automatic.firstPayment`.

For reference, it follows the amount sent in the POST recurring-consents for the first payment:

<div align="center">
  <img src="/uploads/a143f96706d96999017756f9fa05bf0d/image.png" width="300"/>
</div>

Which is later correctly passed in the POST recurring-payments.

Based on this, no changes are needed on the CS side. To a achieve successful execution, the implementation needs to be adjusted to ensure compliance with the specification and the test step mentioned below:

> • Call the POST recurring-payments endpoint **with the information defined firstPayment payload**
>
> • Expects 201 - Validate response

Feel free to open another ticket if you get any other issue! Happy to help!
