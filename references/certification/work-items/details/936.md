## 📝 Descrição

## 1\. What happened and what were you expecting?

The OPF pagination validator is not accepting the pagination-key parameter to be returned at the self-link, a although the specification has this as a compliant behavior to guarantee pagination at operational limits tests.

For the OpfPaginationValidator, it can ignore if the institution add the parameter pagination-key with any value at the self link, accepting this as a compliant behavior.
