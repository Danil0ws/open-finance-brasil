Hi,

We’ve identified that the payment date in your test seems to be set to D+0. For the flow to work as expected, the payment must be scheduled for a future date as it's status is aiming to be SCHD. We’ve just updated the documentation in the wiki to clarify this behavior:

<table> <tr> <td>1400.00</td> <td> Accept consent, accept payment initiation and<strong>, if the payment is for a date later than D+0,</strong> move the payment to the SCHD state _after_ the initiation response has been set, _after_ 3 minutes it will change to CANCELADO_AGENDAMENTO by DETENTORA </td> </tr> </table>

Please note that a payment of 1400.00 set for the same day (D+0) will not transition to the SCHD state, as it doesn’t make sense for a same-day payment to be treated as a scheduled transaction. MB does not override the provided payment date in such cases.

We kindly ask that you adjust the payment date, re-run your test, and let us know if you encounter any further issues.

Happy to help!
