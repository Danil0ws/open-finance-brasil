## 📝 Descrição

## 1. What Happened and what were you expecting?
The `OpfPaginationValidator` is incorrectly expecting the "last" link element to be returned. 
There is not "last" in the BankFixedIncomeLinks object, it has been removed from the specification.

https://openbanking-brasil.github.io/openapi/swagger-apis/bank-fixed-incomes/1.0.0-rc2.0.yml
