## 📝 Descrição

Tester is sending multiple payment calls on payments-api-negative-tests, as pointed on <https://gitlab.com/obb1/certification/-/issues/62#note_714679178> consent must be CONSUMED on first payment call regarding success or failure response.

When tester sends second payment call on same consent testing a different value, we return a CONSENTIMENTO_INVALIDO since consent was CONSUMED and can't be used anymore; tester is expecting VALOR_INCOMPATIVEL or PAGAMENTO_DIVERGENTE_DO_CONSENTIMENTO.

http://web.conformance.directory.openbankingbrasil.org.br/log-detail.html?log=A0rxJijLdBcaAxs
