## 📝 Descrição

## 1\. What happened and what were you expecting?

The credit-cards_api_operational-limits_test-module_v2-1 is trying to find the total number of records returned at the transaction-current endpoint through the totalRecords fields although it does not exist. this is happening because the class `ValidateNumberOfRecordsPage1` is being used, which does this by looking the totalRecords field.

The test modules should try to look at the number of records returned at the data field from the response, instead.

We should also validate if the other operational limits for phase two will be fixed according this behavior, since none of them have the totalRecors field.
