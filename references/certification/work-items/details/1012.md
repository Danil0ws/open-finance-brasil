## 📝 Descrição

## 1. What happened and what were you expecting?
The OpfPaginationValidator expects wrong value at `totalRecords` and `totalPages` when the object `balloonPayments` is not sent at GET /contracts/{contractId}/scheduled-instalments endpoint. When it is not sent, `totalRecords` and `totalPages` should be 0. 

This incorrect current behavior should be reviewed and fixed for the Phase 2 APIs listed below:

* Loans
* Financings
* Invoice-financings
* Unarranged-accounts-overdraft

Below it follows evidence:

* Response do not present `balloonPayments` object, and rightfully so, returns `totalRecords` and `totalPages` as 0:
