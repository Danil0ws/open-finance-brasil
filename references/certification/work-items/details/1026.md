## 1. What happened and what were you expecting?

The test is validating that an expired consent (after authorized) must return the `reason.code` as `CONSENT_EXPIRED`, but according to the [doc](https://openfinancebrasil.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/OF/pages/175833251/Informa+es+T+cnicas+-+Consentimento+-+v2.2.0-beta.1) this reason is for the consents not authorised, after the authorisation must be `CONSENT_MAX_DATE_REACHED`
