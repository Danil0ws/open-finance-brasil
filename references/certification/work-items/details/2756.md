## 📝 Descrição

**Observed behavior** 

The five `fvp-enrollments_api_invalid-{challenge,origin,public-key,rpid,status-sign-options}_open_test-module_v2-2` modules fail at the end of an otherwise-passing run.

The journey completes and every assertion passes. After the rejected path the test calls `fireTestFinished()` and enters finalisation; a redirect submission from the browser (`POST /test/a/.../implicit/...`, same `state` as the original authorization response) then arrives and the test ends FAILED with `java.lang.RuntimeException: runInBackground called after runFinalisationTaskInBackground()` (`TestExecutionManager.java:132`.

---

**Expected behavior** 

A late or duplicate redirect submission arriving after the test has finished/entered finalisation is handled gracefully and does not fail a test whose assertions all passed.
