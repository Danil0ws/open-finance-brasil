## 1. What Happened and what were you expecting?
   As we can see below, the conformance engine warns that the **self link** in pagination metadata should be 2 instead of 1, but the request sent contains no page attribute. In this way is automatically assumed **page=1** and consequently **self link = 1**.

   We understand this to be a bug in conformance engine.
