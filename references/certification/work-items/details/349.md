## 1. What Happened and what were you expecting?
We are facing the same failure on three tests:

- discounted-credit-rights-api-test-v2
- loans-api-test-v2
- financings-api-test-v2

It says this fields are not validated on the Warranties response: _links, self, meta, totalRecords, totalPages, requestDateTime_. However, for all three APIs, those fields are required on the warranties endpoint.

We are not sure why we are getting this error message. In our understanding, it is an unexpected behavior.
