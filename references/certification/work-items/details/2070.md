The issue occurs because of an extra space before the " DE" in the feeCode field.

Although the value appears to match the expected pattern, any leading spaces at the beginning of the string will cause a validation failure.

Please ensure that the feeCode value does not contain any unnecessary spaces at the beginning or end.
