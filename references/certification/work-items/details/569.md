The Conformance Suite makes both GET Consents and GET Payments calls using the self-link sent on the respective POST Request. In the tests mentioned, the call are being the endpoints mentioned since they are being sent on the links for the POST Response:

    "self": "https://obb-mtls-sandbox.banrisul.com.br/open-banking/payments/v2/pix/payments/20230106ruC1uOzCttC/20230106ruC1uOzCttC"
