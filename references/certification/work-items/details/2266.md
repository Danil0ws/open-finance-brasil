### 1. Observed Behavior

At the time of execution, the scheduled test at [FVP Scheduler](https://scheduler.fvp.directory.openbankingbrasil.org.br/?continue) under Schedule ID `ab0d6268-da3c-41ba-b08d-392235d36407` failed with status `ERROR`, as shown in the image below:

![image.png](/uploads/f396961b5fd0b392dca2bef3fdb180cf/image.png){width=1554 height=154}

### 2. Expected Behavior

The scheduled test should transition to status `COMPLETED` at the specified execution time.

### 3. Evidence

* **Schedule ID:** `ab0d6268-da3c-41ba-b08d-392235d36407`

### 5. Steps to Reproduce
