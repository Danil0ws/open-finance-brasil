## 📝 Descrição


#### Current Behavior:
---
The second payment is working even with the `payment.amount` (0.2 BRL) is lower than the `minimumVariableAmount` (0.5 BRL).

#### Expected Behavior:
---
When the `paymen.amount` is lower than the `minimumVariableAmount` defined in the consent creation it should throw an exception.

Hi @lucas.barros7

Thanks for creating the issue!

This matter has been discussed in the following other issue that got a response from the Work Group: https://gitlab.com/raidiam-conformance/open-finance/certification/-/issues/2031

As described by the WG in that issue, the minimumVariableAmount does not limit the minimum amount sent during payment. The purpose of this field is to establish a minimum range from the the payer can chose a maximumVariableAmount.

Therefore, an exception would only be expected if the maximumVariableAmount is lower then the minimumVariableAmount, and this would only happen during Post recurring-consents.

Feel free add new comments in case further support is needed,

Happy to help!
