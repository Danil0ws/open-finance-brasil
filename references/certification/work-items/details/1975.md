The test failed because your implementation is missing mandatory field `data.recurringConfiguration.sweeping.useOverdraftLimit` in the 201 response for POST recurring-consents:

The error is better shown below:

![image](/uploads/0e08a02574e63ac67b4c86a4e9fa70b4/image.png){width="500"}

It follows specification ([API Automatic Payments v2.0.0-beta.2](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/OpenBanking-Brasil/openapi/main/swagger-apis/automatic-payments/2.0.0-beta.2.yml)) reference:

![image](https://gitlab.com/-/project/26426113/uploads/2427df0169ec404ea5c1b1e1c9414d86/image.png){width="500"}
