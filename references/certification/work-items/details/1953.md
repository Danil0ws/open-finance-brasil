The test failed because your implementation is missing the mandatory field `data.recurringConfiguration.sweeping.useOverdraftLimit` in the 201 response for POST recurring-consents. The error is better shown below:

![image](/uploads/5983eeafe4175f0891de25b9dd622ec3/image.png){width="500"}

It follows specification ([API Automatic Payments v2.0.0-beta.2](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/OpenBanking-Brasil/openapi/main/swagger-apis/automatic-payments/2.0.0-beta.2.yml)) reference:

![image](/uploads/b3e61f83c5e31374b219c123bd1906b1/image.png){width="700"}

Therefore, no corrections are needed on the CS side. To ensure successful execution, the implementation must include all mandatory fields in the responses.
