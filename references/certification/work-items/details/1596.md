Hi,

Thanks for bringing this to our attention!

We have reviewed the situation and would like to update you on the following:

1. **Adjustment to the Conformance Suite:** We will modify the conformance suite so that the **`meta`** field is not required for successful test execution. This update will align the test suite’s behavior with the v2 documentation, where **`meta`** is marked as optional.
2. **For WG Evaluation on Meta and Links Fields:**
   * We have observed that when the fields provided in a response are not subject to pagination, the inclusion of **`meta`** and **`links`** fields may not be necessary. This observation raises a question about their optional status and whether they should be included in the specifications at all to prevent ambiguity.
   * To address this, we would like WG evaluation to determine if the **`meta`** field should be included in the specification even if as optional element, or if it should be omitted entirely.

Please let us know if you have any further observations or require additional assistance.

Happy to help!
