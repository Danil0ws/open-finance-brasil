## 1. What Happened and what were you expecting?

In the token request with grant_type **client_credentials**, the client scope is being requested. It is our understanding that the request for data consumption scopes should not be allowed for the **grant_type client_credentials**, for token creation with the customer scope, the client's authorization is required.
