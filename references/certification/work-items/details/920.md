## 1. What happened and what were you expecting?
The `payments_api_x-fapi_test-module_v3` should have a polling sequence at the second GET Payments (_Call GET Payments with the x-fapi-interaction-id_) until it receives the status `SCHD` and then call PATCH Payments.

Because it does not have it, the test mentioned below fails as PATCH Payments is called when the payment is still at `RCVD` status.
