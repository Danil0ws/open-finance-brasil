Following the development analysis of the logs for the tests you mentioned, we indeed identified that the conformance engine received **522 error (Connection Timed Out)** responses when attempting to access the following URLs during test execution:

* `https://openfinance.carrefoursolucoes.com.br/atacadao/.well-known/openid-configuration`
* `https://openfinance.carrefoursolucoes.com.br/carrefour/.well-known/openid-configuration`
* `https://openfinance.carrefoursolucoes.com.br/samsclub/.well-known/openid-configuration`

A 522 error generally indicates a connectivity issue between your origin server and the reverse proxy/CDN service (such as Cloudflare, if applicable).

It is important to note that when we attempted to reproduce the reported behavior by accessing these same links from our environment, **we could not identify any access problems**, and the endpoints responded correctly. Furthermore, we have not received reports of unavailability or similar errors from other participants on the platform.

Given this, and considering the intermittent nature that a 522 error can present, we kindly request that you **investigate your infrastructure**. It is possible that some intermittent unavailability or timeout is occurring on your end.

After verifying and making any necessary adjustments to your infrastructure, we ask that you **run the tests again**.

Feel free to reopen the ticket or open a new one if you require additional support.
