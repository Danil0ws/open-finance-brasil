## 📝 Descrição

## 1\. What happened and what were you expecting?

The loans_api_operational-limits_test-module_v2-1 incorrectly calls `GetLoansPaymentsValidatorV2n` to validate a `GET /contracts/{contractID}/warranties` response, causing the error below to happen:

![MicrosoftTeams-image (54).png](/uploads/20504832f75a593d2df5db8f89e27006/MicrosoftTeams-image__54_.png)

The Warranties endpoint response indeed should present a JsonArray, as it is being sent on the example above.

Swagger reference: [API Loans 2.1.0-rc.1.0](https://openbanking-brasil.github.io/openapi/swagger-apis/loans/2.1.0-rc.1.yml)
