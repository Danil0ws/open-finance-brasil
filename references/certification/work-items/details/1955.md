#### Current Behavior:

The test anticipates that the updatedAtDateTime will be a future date and time relative to the time of the patch request, taking milliseconds into account.

#### Expected Behavior:

The test should not consider milliseconds (not the spec pattern). Therefore, the expected updatedAtDateTime should be at least the same as the time of the request.

The same thing happens on test **automatic-payments_api_automatic-pix-consent-edition-restrictive_test-module_v2**.
