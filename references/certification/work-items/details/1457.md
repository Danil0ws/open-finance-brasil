## 📝 Descrição

## 1\. What happened and what were you expecting?

According to the test module, after creating a consent of amount value as 600 and 2 payments of 300, the suit expects consent to be CONSUMED. 

![image.png](/uploads/10e48ecd07bd47a8b49a9534c8119ced/image.png)However, consent status will never be CONSUMED. 

![image.png](/uploads/3f28b3844501b6492693ce5946f59507/image.png)

 This will happen because the Consent has only a transactionLimit, rather than totalAllowedAmount. 

![image.png](/uploads/9aaebd42b638e9d1225cc6f1b46141e5/image.png)

![image.png](/uploads/682e816d336ead4371d1199ae1f3b904/image.png)

The only 2 conditions that would change the consent status to CONSUMED are explained below:

![image.png](/uploads/9193f63931d6c7b83eb0f92f8dcf6522/image.png)Please consider changing the payload, sending a sweeping of totalAllowedAmount 600 rather than a transactionLimit of 600. That way this functionality will be properly tested.
