## 📝 Descrição

#### Current Behavior:

---

We would like your guidance regarding the test **automatic-payments_api_automatic-pix-failed-firstPayment_test-module**.

**Context:** The test expects the membership payment (**firstPayment**) to fail, but the authorization should still be created, allowing the scheduling of a payment in the **Automatic Pix** context.

However, this behavior conflicts with the expected behavior of **Automatic Pix** outside **Open Finance**. As seen on pages **19, 20, and 21** of the **BCB Automatic Pix Implementation Guide** ([link](https://www.bcb.gov.br/content/estabilidadefinanceira/pix/guia_pix_automatico.pdf)), authorization is conditioned on the success of the first payment.

Should the behavior of the same product be different in these two contexts (**Automatic Pix within and outside Open Finance**)?

#### Expected Behavior:

---

Explain the behavior you were expecting to see instead. How do you think the system should behave?

Hi @GutembergTeros

Thank you for reaching out!

Currently, the failure of the first payment does not cancel the consent, allowing subsequent payments to be scheduled within the Open Finance context. In Open Finance, the consent remains valid even if the first payment fails. This decision was made by the WorkGroup to align with the consent model and authorization management framework of the Open Finance ecosystem.

![image.png](/uploads/e2f77911736b9048183b2d0c5bff3ab0/image.png)

If there are any concerns regarding this behavior, we recommend opening a ticket directed to Chicago for further evaluation. If you need further clarification, we are available to assist.

Let us know if you need further clarification.
