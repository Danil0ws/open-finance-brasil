Hello @felipe.techera1. Thanks for the extra context and for confirming this was not a certification test plan scenario but a QA flow with your TPP.

In your payload, the account/document data you sent does not match the CPF/CNPJ linked to the consent, so the ownership check fails first, and the Mock Bank returns the rejectReason "Titularidade inconsistente". Because that validation fails earlier in the flow, the balance check is not reached, and you will not see "Saldo insuficiente" for this exact request.

Please double-check the identification fields you are sending (cnpjInitiator, creditorAccount, document) to make sure they are consistent with the consent information.

If you want to exercise the `Saldo insuficiente` case in QA:

- You need to use account/consent data that passes the ownership and account-data checks, and
- Then configure the amount so that it exceeds the configured balance/limit for that scenario.

A practical option is to mirror the personal information included on the mock bank pre-configured JSONs at the bottom of the page and then adapt it for your QA tests.
