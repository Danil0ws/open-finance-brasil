## 📝 Descrição

Hi guys,

While validating the information in section 9.4 regarding signature certificate validation (353), I would like to confirm my understanding of this point:

"9.4. Signature certificates validation
The directory uses cert rescan function hourly to validation process"

In situations where there is a sync issue, we cache the CRL and OCSP responses for as long as the response is valid, as indicated by the expiry or nextUpdate fields, with a maximum time of 7 days.

Could you please confirm if this differs from what is expected by OpenFinance Certificate Validation?

Hi,

As noted by you point [9.4 of the documentation] states the behaviour taken by the directory when doing certificate validation, which is an architecture decision taken by Raidiam, the directory service provider. 

As the Directory is the central piece of trust of the Ecosystem at Raidiam we consider that we cannot take risks over displaying revoked private_keys, hence why, if the OCSP and CRL are unavailable when the hourly check is done, it will consider the keys to be inactivated. 

For participants, on the other hand, there's no formal definition of how this should be dealt with by the Ecosystem participants and as such, the behaviour that you take should be defined mainly by your internal Security Policy.
