Hi @christianraidiam!

   In the specification is mentioned that **PARAMETRO_INVALIDO** is used for format of params. In this case the dates are correctly formated.
 
   In other hand, **DATA_PAGAMENTO_INVALIDA** code, is used when the date is correctly formated but is not valid for the form of payment.

   In the test mentioned, the conformance engine try to send two dates with same values (day, month and year).

  ![Screenshot_from_2024-01-30_10-10-20](/uploads/4d5748c1af0f22ae7bc0f97a766da008/Screenshot_from_2024-01-30_10-10-20.png)
