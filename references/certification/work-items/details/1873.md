Thanks for your patience. Glad to hear the error isn’t happening anymore.

To answer your question: our limit is around 1 request per second. Exceeding this may be flagged as potential DDoS activity, as Mock Bank is intended for functional testing, not high-volume traffic. This limit is important to ensure stability for all MB users. If an institution exceeds the limit, their IP will be temporarily blocked and automatically unblocked after a certain period. 

We understand that you’re currently testing consumer products and to prevent this error from happening again, we suggest using the Conformance Suite, which includes APIs and bypasses WAF filtering.

As this issue is now resolved, I’ll proceed to close it. Don’t hesitate to reach out if you need further assistance. Happy to help!
