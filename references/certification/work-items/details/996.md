## 1. What happened and what were you expecting?
The 3.0.0-rc.1 documentation is clear when stating, in the ["Controle de Acesso"](https://openfinancebrasil.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/OF/pages/164528425/Informa+es+T+cnicas+-+Pagamentos+-+v3.0.0-rc.1) section, that the account provider can choose if the QRCode validations are done in the consent creation or in the payment flow.

![image](/uploads/afdbf9e9f32c8f653198bd6c0a0c5003/image.png)

However, there are 4 tests modules that are not in accordance with the specification above as they are enforcing this validation to be made only in the consent flow, causing a failure in our tests as our application was build to validate it in the payments flow. 

Also, as stated in #991 and #990, we shouldn't be enforcing business rules in a security layer as we believe it is a distortion of its purpose and roles.
