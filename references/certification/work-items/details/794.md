## 1. What Happened and what were you expecting?
The CS is incorrectly expecting the resource type for the funds_api_resources_test-module to be "FUNDS". As shown below in the specification, it should be "FUND".

                type: string
                enum:
                  - ACCOUNT
                  - CREDIT_CARD_ACCOUNT
                  - LOAN
                  - FINANCING
                  - UNARRANGED_ACCOUNT_OVERDRAFT
                  - INVOICE_FINANCING
                  - BANK_FIXED_INCOME
                  - CREDIT_FIXED_INCOME
                  - VARIABLE_INCOME
                  - TREASURE_TITLE
                  - FUND
