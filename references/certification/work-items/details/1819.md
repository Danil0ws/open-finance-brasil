Hi Henrique! Thanks for the ticket!

As defined at the [JSR specification](https://openfinancebrasil.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/OF/pages/591102997/Informa+es+T+cnicas+-+SV+V+nculo+de+dispositivo+-+v2.0.0-rc.1), the challenge at the fido registration optoins endpoint should be returned without padding:

![image.png](/uploads/698583648085862add48275c49676a86/image.png){width=522 height=64}

In the challenge sent, although it's a compliant base64, it's has a padding at the end: `eGllRHNUNTlqanVvYnVXY2tHWFlSakMvYnUzT0Z3RW5mNUNIcythUXErTT0`**`=`**

In that sense, there's no correction at the conformance suite. To have a sucessegul test execution, please send the challgende without th padding.

We are happy to help with any additional needs!
