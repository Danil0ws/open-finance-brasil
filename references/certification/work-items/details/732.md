Hi,

Thanks for the ticket! 

One of the ways the tests know which scopes should be requested is based on the `consentUrl` sent by the participant. In your case, the `consentUrl` sent follows the regex: `^(https://)(.*?)(consents/v[0-9]/consents)` which causes the test to require `consents` scope. 

Therefore, there is no corrections on the CS side. To a successful execution, the implementation needs to be adapted so that the `consentUrl` does not suggest `consents` scope to be used. In other words, for the test to require only payments scopes, your consentUrl should be adapted accordingly to follow the regex: `^(https://)(.*?)(payments/v[0-9]/consents)`

Feel free to open another ticket if you get any other issue! Happy to help!
