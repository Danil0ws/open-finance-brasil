@john.oliveira.lords

According to [RFC6749](https://datatracker.ietf.org/doc/html/rfc6749)

[**A.17**](https://datatracker.ietf.org/doc/html/rfc6749#appendix-A.17)**. "refresh_token" Syntax**

The "refresh_token" element is defined in Sections [5.1](https://datatracker.ietf.org/doc/html/rfc6749#section-5.1) and [6](https://datatracker.ietf.org/doc/html/rfc6749#section-6):

refresh-token = 1\*VSCHAR

Which means:

• refresh-token must be composed of one or more visible characters (VSCHAR).

Therefore, it is my understanding that the notation `1*VSCHAR` indicates that the token must consist of at least one visible character, but it does not impose any maximum length. Although the size of the refresh token may be constrained by storage limits, transmission protocols, or specific implementation details that might impose their own limits.
