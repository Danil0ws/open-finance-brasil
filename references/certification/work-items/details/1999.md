## 📝 Descrição

#### Current Behavior:

Payload sent on POST Payments:
```
"date": "2025-01-19",
"paymentReference": "W4-2025"
```

But when we check the week of this date on [wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ISO_8601)

![image](/uploads/17701ef43bd68a2618c9eb432558d080/image.png){width=848 height=231}

2025-01-19 it is on week number 3
---

#### Expected Behavior:
---
Should be sent "paymentReference": "W3-2025" on POST Payments payload.
