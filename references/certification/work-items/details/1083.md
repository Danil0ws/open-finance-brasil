## 1. What happened and what were you expecting?

Hello, I experienced unexpected behavior while conducting the test

payments_api_multiple-consents-conditional_test-module_v3

When the applicant carries out the consent (first part), they are expecting the consent to return as AUTHORISED instead of AWAITING_AUTHORISATION.

Following the expected behavior of the flow, the correct procedure would be to wait for the consent in AWAITING_AUTHORISATION, until the approver makes their approval move, and then the consent can change to the next steps.

Hi Jorge! Thanks for the ticket!

Looking at the Conformance suite logs, the tests is expecting the Authorized status for the consent after the redirect, which is the expected behavior. 

For multiple consents accounts, after the first authorizer grants the consent, the consent should go to AUTHORIZED status. The payments, then, should be kept at PATC until the second authorized grants the consent.

In that sense, there is no fix to be executed in the conformance suite side, since the consent should be at Authorized status after the redirect.

We are happpy to helo with any additional needs.
