## 1. What Happened and what were you expecting?

When the test removes the webhook endpoints it goes straight into the mode of waiting a Payment to be confirmed, however this conflicts with the specification as Payment can still be on RCVD state.

Here we should have a Polling sequence as all the others tests, as the lack of webhook does mean that we can't know when payment status will be set
