## 📝 Descrição

On the Resources test plan, you are asking the users to consent for accounts and credit-cards permissions on the following tests:
resources-api-test-accounts
resources-api-test-credit-card

Not all institutions will have those two products deployed therefore those two tests should not be mandatory when requesting certification. 

When implementing this change you also need to take into account how to communicate to the end-user that those tests are not mandatory. 

I think (but i am not sure) another option here would be to maybe move those two tests to the accounts and credit cards test, as someone who is testing those APIs necessarily has to have implemented the resources APIs.

What we have discussed on our development is the following:

On the creation of the resources Test Plan

1- The org will select which resources they have implemented ("Credit Cards, Credit Ops, Accounts) and provide the corresponding endpoints
2- The resources test will be comprised of a set of tests that match all resources that they may test (e.g. resources-accounts)
3- If the org selected that they don't have a resource implemented they will either "auto pass the test" or the test will be disabled by them
4- This will be registered on their logs and so we can check if orgs have lied.
