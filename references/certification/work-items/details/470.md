## 1. What Happened and what were you expecting?
We have a brand that supports only financings, so when a request is sent to consent endpoint with permissions only for other products, like accounts ou credit cards, it returns a 422 as no valid permission group is left. Operational limits tests for accounts and credit cards fails in this situation and it should end with a warning.
Also, even seting up personal products, customer business test fails if we don't provide credentials for that.
