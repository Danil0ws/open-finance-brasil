## 📝 Descrição

## 1\. What happened and what were you expecting?

According to specification, the AUTHORISED status has two possible paths:

1. Going from PARTIALLY_ACCEPTED to AUTHORISED (required)
2. Going from AWAITING_AUTHORISATION to AUTHORISED (optional)

The specification states that on case 1 the webhook call is required, while the second case is optional and decided by the institution. We want to call the webhook on both cases, but I'm getting an error in all the tests while this worked for Automatic Payments V1 webhook plan.

We call 2 webhooks for consent, one for AUTHORISED and another for CONSUMED. I suspect this is what is causing the test to fail:

![image.png](/uploads/1af148cfe0a3e92dc6c65a386b8368c3/image.png)

[Pagamentos - Webhook - v1.2.0 - Área do Desenvolvedor - Open Finance Brasil - Área do Desenvolvedor (atlassian.net)](https://openfinancebrasil.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/OF/pages/200737002/Pagamentos+-+Webhook+-+v1.2.0#M%C3%A1quinas-de-estados\[hardBreak\]\[hardBreak\])

`O status AUTHORISED deverá disparar notificações de acordo com a condição abaixo:`

* `Caso o consentimento tenha atingido o status AUTHORISED após ter transitado pelo status PARTIALLY_ACCEPTED, o detentor deve notificar o Iniciador via Webhook;`
* `Caso o consentimento tenha transitado diretamente para AUTHORISED, é facultada ao detentor a notificação.`

Can you please verify if this is the problem?
