Marcelo, 

Looking at your error message i can only assume that you are expecting a transaction ID inside the QRES provided by the conformance suite. PIX_REQ_TXID_ERROR

Statis QR Codes do not mandate the transactionid to be sent, instead a *** can be sent on position 62 as per https://www.bcb.gov.br/content/estabilidadefinanceira/forumpireunioes/AnexoI-PadroesParaIniciacaodoPix.pdf

If this is not your issue i ask you to please provide the test plan in which you found the error together with more details about why do you think the Conformacne Suite is wrong.
