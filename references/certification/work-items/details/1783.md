Hi All,

Although the PNDG status can be reached, it is meant for situations where the payment is under risk analysis. In the certification test modules, there are specific tests designed to assess risk analysis parameters. In other tests, the PNDG status should not be triggered, and therefore, a webhook should not be expected.

We are happy to assist with any additional needs!
