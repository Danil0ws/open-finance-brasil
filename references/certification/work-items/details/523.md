## 1. What Happened and what were you expecting?

Hi Mauricio and Matheus!
Thanks for the ticket!

According to [PIX documentation](https://www.bcb.gov.br/content/estabilidadefinanceira/pix/Regulamento_Pix/II_ManualdePadroesparaIniciacaodoPix.pdf):

- Conforme EMV QRCPS–MPM QR Codes for Payment Systems – Merchant Presented Mode, seção 4.8.1.2: “If present, the content of the data object value for IDs "01" to "08" shall be either *** or a value defined by the merchant. The presence of *** indicates that the mobile application is responsible for obtaining the necessary information”. Conclui-se que, se o gerador do QR optar por não utilizar um transactionID, o valor ‘***’ deverá ser usado para indicar essa escolha.

In that sense, the value *** should not be considered as the value for txID, instead, it means that the field is not present. Therefore, since the txID is not being sent, we are also not sending the transactionIdentifer on the POST Payments Payload when localInstrument is QRES, as defined by Phase 3v2 specifications.

In a nutshell, the value *** means that the field is not present, and does need to follow the regex for the transactionIdentifies specified at the swagger.

Please, feel free to reopen the ticket if you think the issue remains. Happy to help with any other issue.
