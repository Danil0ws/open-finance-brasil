Hello, @hudson.ishibashi1 and @gimendes. Thanks for bringing this out.

Regarding the initial issue: the failure was caused by the **test** **configuration**. You have selected Personal Products (instead of Business Products) and left brazilCnpj empty. With this configuration, the test builds the consent using a personal context, which will conflict with the Pix Automático business rules.

![image.png](/uploads/8921ed1752db2470b292997cb9c58b61/image.png){width=796 height=484}

To validate the expected Pix Automático scenario, we updated your configuration to match the spec intent and re-ran the test:

* Business Products
* brazilCnpj filled in

As a result, `creditors` was set as PESSOA_JURIDICA with a CNPJ (single item in the array).

![image.png](/uploads/c1ecaa129aff1da76659c0a21fd3ebb5/image.png){width=353 height=158}

After applying these changes, the payload aligns with the Pix Automático rules (single creditor + PJ/CNPJ). 

However, your AS returned a **new error**, unrelated to the previous configuration issue:

![image.png](/uploads/3f5f1e41b3ec3834381a88155deecee4/image.png){width=623 height=158}

The AS now returns PARAMETRO_INVALIDO, stating that the `journey` parameter does not comply with the expected formatting rules. However, the journey object the Conformance Suite sent includes the two fields required by the spec (`isLinked` and `linkId`) and they appear to be formatted according to the specification. The AS response does not indicate which specific validation rule (or field) is failing.

So, in summary:

1. The original problem (Personal vs Business / missing brazilCnpj) was **configuration-related**
2. Once the configuration is corrected, the remaining failure points to an **AS-side validation issue on** `journey`
