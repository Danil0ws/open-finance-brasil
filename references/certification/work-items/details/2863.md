## 📝 Descrição

Hi Master,

We are testing the Open Finance Brasil Mock Bank Sandbox Payments v5 Pix payment flow from an Android native application.

#### Participant:

`7844e311-aa1f-4f67-9475-cbd989310b3e` — Mock Bank Sandbox

#### Problem:

The Android FIDO enrollment flow completes successfully. However, when creating a Pix payment, Mock Bank Sandbox first accepts the payment with HTTP 201 and status `RCVD`, then the payment immediately changes to `RJCT` with:

```json
{
  "code": "PAGAMENTO_RECUSADO_DETENTORA",
  "detail": "PAGAMENTO_RECUSADO_DETENTORA"
}
```

This happens with different payment amounts, so it does not appear to be caused by a specific amount.
#### Could you please confirm:
1. Is the immediate transition from RCVD to RJCT/PAGAMENTO_RECUSADO_DETENTORA expected in Mock Bank Sandbox?
2. Does the Sandbox test CPF/debtor account require an additional payment approval, FIDO signing step, or a specific test action after payment creation?
3. Are there specific Sandbox test accounts, consent settings, or payment data required to obtain a successful Pix payment status such as ACSC?
4. Is there an official and current document describing Mock Bank Sandbox limitations and test data for Payments v5, including:
   - supported test CPF and debtor accounts;
   - payment amount or transaction-limit rules;
   - required approval/FIDO steps after payment creation;
   - deterministic scenarios that result in `ACSC` versus `RJCT`?
#### Concern:
The payment request is accepted successfully, but it is rejected by the Sandbox immediately afterwards. We cannot complete an end-to-end successful Pix payment validation after completing the FIDO flow.
#### Evidence:

Hi @zhidong.tan,

This is expected Mock Bank behaviour, not a defect. The 8 characters after `E` in the `endToEndId` must identify the party that generated the id (the payment initiator), not the creditor.

In your payments:

- `endToEndId` = `E99999008...` -\> the 8 characters are `99999008`, which is the creditor account ISPB (`creditorAccount.ispb`), i.e. the wrong party.
- The initiator that generated the id is your `cnpjInitiator` = `43142666000197`.

The Mock Bank checks those 8 characters against the first 8 digits of your `cnpjInitiator` (`43142666`). `99999008` is not `43142666`, so it rejects with `PAGAMENTO_RECUSADO_DETENTORA`, regardless of amount.

**Fix:** put the initiator's identifier in the `endToEndId`, i.e. the first 8 digits of your `cnpjInitiator`: `E43142666` + `YYYYMMDDHHmm` + 11 alphanumerics (for example `E43142666202609041230abcdefghijk`). Then the payment reaches `ACSC`. No extra FIDO or approval step is involved.

**Basis:** the `endToEndId` is generated by the initiator, and its 8-character field identifies that generating party (Open Finance Brasil Payment Initiation spec, `endToEndId` field: https://openfinancebrasil.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/OF/pages/1767146337). For a payment initiator, that identification is the first 8 digits of its CNPJ ([Manual de Padrões para Iniciação do Pix, BCB, section 2.1.1](https://www.bcb.gov.br/content/estabilidadefinanceira/pix/Regulamento_Pix/II_ManualdePadroesparaIniciacaodoPix.pdf)). The creditor account ISPB is a different field and is not used in this check.

If you run into any other issue, feel free to reach out to us.
