We would like to understand from an upper-level perspective how do you plan to store or show that a given authorization server has been certified on the directory.

Please consider here that we will mandate the certification for every issuer on the well-known, as recommended by the OIDF.

Also consider here that we must guarantee that the A.S. won't be visible if it has not been certified

As we have spoken on the 24/05 I understand that your proposal here is that the Authorization Server will have a new object which will contain information related to the that has been issued for that given A.S.

The directory won't block any org to publish any A.S. it will be the job of a TPP to decide if it will or not connect with a non-certified A.S.

The IN98 states that orgs may not register their A.S. on production if they have not been certified, enforcing this policy will be made by the central banks. The job of the directory here is to inform the TPPs and the Central Bank
