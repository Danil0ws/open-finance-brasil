Hello @zhidong.tan, thanks for reaching out!

The Mock Bank's webhook behavior for enrollments follows the Open Finance Brasil specification. According to the\
https://openfinancebrasil.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/OF/pages/1436549234:

▎ (PT) "Os eventos que acionarão notificações na máquina de estados do vínculo de conta da jornada sem redirecionamento serão os status REJECTED e REVOKED"

▎ (EN) "The events that will trigger notifications in the enrollment state machine for the no-redirect journey are the REJECTED and REVOKED statuses"

This means the transition to AUTHORISED does not trigger a webhook notification.

Regarding your questions:

- Was the webhook URI successfully registered? Yes! It was successfully registered at 09:57:05 UTC for client hwmNDEQ4YD8kRsWIgN1AZ.
- Did the Mock Bank attempt any webhook deliveries? No, and this is the expected behavior, since AUTHORISED is not a webhook-triggering event.

Hope this clarifies things! Let us know if you have any further questions.
