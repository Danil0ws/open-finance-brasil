Thanks for the ticket!

The test failed because your implementation is sending the field `data.recurringConfiguration.automatic.referenceStartDateTime` with a pattern that doesn't match the required pattern on the 200 response for GET recurring-consents. The error is better shown below:

![image](/uploads/ffb946cd00d728d8cdcd4b4128b72d72/image.png){width="500"}

It follows specification ([API Automatic Payments v2.0.0-beta.2](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/OpenBanking-Brasil/openapi/main/swagger-apis/automatic-payments/2.0.0-beta.2.yml)) reference:

![image](/uploads/7135e8ad3f4febcf4927907211bd8de1/image.png){width="500"}

Therefore, no corrections are needed on the CS side. To ensure successful execution, the implementation must follow the specification defined patterns in the responses.

Feel free to open another ticket if you get any other issue! Happy to help!
