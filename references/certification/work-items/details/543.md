Hi Jaqueline! Thanks for the ticket!

The test is expecting that the account will eventually have not enough funds to accomplish the requested payment. In that sense, even if the account holder does offer an alternative account, that should be done on the redirect for consent authorization, and the error message on POST payments will be kept as the test module requires.

Therefore, the test module does not have any different behavior than specified at the UX guide.
Please, feel free to reopen the ticket if you think the issue remains.
