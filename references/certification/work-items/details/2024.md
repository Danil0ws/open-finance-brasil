Hi @doylesm 

Thank you for reaching out regarding the issue with the automatic-payments_api_sweeping-accounts-wrong-creditor_test-module_v2, which is throwing a java.lang.NullPointerException.

Upon analysis, the error seems to occur because the provided proxy in the request does not meet the requirements outlined in the specification. Specifically, the proxy field must adhere to the validation rules specified for the **DICT localInstrument**, and the detentora must **validate** the proxy in the DICT and the creditorAccount object.

"rejectionReason": {
      "code": "PAGAMENTO_RECUSADO_DETENTORA",
      "detail": "Registro de chave de endereçamento a83f13ef-6571-4442-aa38-1c993425bb7e não encontrado."

**Key Points from the Specification:**
The proxy field must be a valid key registered in the DICT and match the expected formats (e.g., CPF, CNPJ, email, phone number, or random UUID as per RFC4122).

When localInstrument is DICT, the proxy field is mandatory and must be validated against the DICT.
If the proxy is not found in the DICT or is invalid, the detentora is expected to reject the payment with a status of RJCT and a rejection reason such as PAGAMENTO_RECUSADO_DETENTORA.

body:
