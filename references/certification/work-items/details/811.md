## 📝 Descrição

Hi, I have a question about what attributes should be included in the DCR response when webhooks are involved. This is related to another issue that was raised here: https://gitlab.com/raidiam-conformance/open-finance/certification/-/issues/805

In reference to `19. If the webhook_uris field is not declared in the payload, the webhook functionality shall be considered disabled for the specific client.`, what does "payload" refer to? It is just the DCR request or also the response? 

The reason I'm asking is because it is possible that the authorization server and the account holder could be two separate entities. In this case, it would make sense that `webhook_uris` is a required field in the DCR/DCM response so that someone is able to determine whether webhooks are enabled or disabled, as this cannot be determined by just looking at the registered software statement. 

I'm unable to find supporting documentation that this is required, but the test in the issue I linked has an assertion for that field being absent:

Hi Dylan,

"Payload" on the documentation refers to the Request_Body Payload sent on the DCR - POST /registration request.

What you suggest on your comment is around what should be the response_body of the POST/GET requests, which should be defined by the DCR swagger - https://github.com/OpenBanking-Brasil/specs-seguranca/blob/main/dcr_review/dcr-dcm-swagger.yaml

When looking at the .yaml file above we can see that this is not an expected field. As here you are suggesting a change on the specifications, rather than an update on tests. For this specific behaviour i would ask you to raise a ticket on the [Open Finance Service Desk](https://servicedesk.openfinancebrasil.org.br/Login.jsp?navLanguage=en-US) suggesting a change on the API
