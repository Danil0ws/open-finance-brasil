## 1. What happened and what were you expecting?
Dear,
We are refusing to create consents because we validate the startDateTime field, if it is a future date, as per restriction in the technical documentation. We would like support in understanding if the validation done by our FI is invalid or if the conformance engine is sending an invalid date.

We believe that the conformance engine is sending the value "date+hour+minutes+seconds"(now) in the recurring-consents post to the "startDateTime" field, but when the request arrives at our IF, this date is already in the past, because it took some time between the conformance engine request reaching our FI.

The documentation contains the following restriction for the "startDateTime" field:
string($date-time)
example: 2021-05-21T08:30:00Z
pattern: ^(\d{4})-(1[0-2]|0?[1-9])-(3[01]|[12][0-9]|0?[1-9] )T(?:[01]\d|2[0123]):(?:[012345]\d):(?:[012345]\d)Z$
maxLength: 20
Date and time at which consent must become valid. A string with date and time according to the RFC-3339 specification, always using UTC timezone (UTC time format).

[Restriction] Mandatory completion when payment consent becomes valid on a future date (day and time).
Reference: "https://openfinancebrasil.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/OF/pages/345178397/Informa+es+T+cnicas+-+SV+Pagamentos+Autom+ticos+-+v1.0.0"

Hi Lucas! Thanks for reaching out with your ticket.

I've looked into the concerns regarding the `startDateTime` validation within the recurring consents process. Based on the technical specifications you've referenced and our subsequent analysis, it appears there might have been some confusion regarding the handling of the `startDateTime` field.

The `startDateTime` field is indeed validated against the RFC-3339 specification, mandating a UTC format for date and time. This field is crucial for indicating the moment consent becomes valid, which aligns with the documentation you've cited. However, it's important to note that the consent's start time does not necessarily need to be in the future. It's primarily meant to indicate when the consent starts, and while it's mandatory for consents that become valid at a future date, it can also reflect a past or current time to accommodate the processing delay between the conformance engine's request and its reception by your Financial Institution (FI).

This understanding was clarified in discussions regarding the `startDateTime` field, leading to it being made optional in the latest versions of the documentation. The flexibility in handling the `startDateTime` allows the initiating party to send it as "now" (indicating the current time) or to omit the field if the consent is meant to be immediate.

Therefore, the behavior you're experiencing, where the `startDateTime` might sometimes be in the past by the time it's processed, is considered valid. This adjustment was made to ensure the field accommodates real-world scenarios more effectively, acknowledging the time it takes for requests to propagate through the system.

We appreciate your diligence in seeking clarification on this matter. Should there be any more questions or if further assistance is needed, please don't hesitate to reach out. 

We are happy to help with any additional needs!
