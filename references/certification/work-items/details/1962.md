## 📝 Descrição

#### Current Behavior:
---

In `automatic-payments_api_sweeping-accounts-consent-edition_test-module_v2` a PATCH recurring-consents is made lacking the mandatory field `data.creditors`. The error is shown below:

![image](/uploads/1d67a96a93365369065ea64a3405b94c/image.png)


#### Expected Behavior:
---

According to the specification ([API Automatic Payments v2.0.0-beta.2](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/OpenBanking-Brasil/openapi/main/swagger-apis/automatic-payments/2.0.0-beta.2.yml)), the PATCH recurring-consents should include the field `data.creditors` in the payload to avoid throwing an error different from the one expected.
![image](/uploads/3a6be579eef0175cbd9afbcf8cd98fed/image.png){width=1379 height=362}
