## 📝 Descrição

## 1. What happened and what were you expecting?
By the specification definition, during assynchronous scenarios the payment transactions should have 60 minutes to reach the SCHD state on each payment, but the test give us only 30 seconds after POST /pix/payments request, to start the polling sequence, which is not enough for the first payment to be scheduled. During this test, the timer delay for the polling sequence could be longer, or maybe do some retries if it identifies that payment is in PNDG state.
