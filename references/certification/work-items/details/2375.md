Hello @170791daniel. Thanks for the report.

Just to clarify, the `paymentReference` value sent in POST /recurring-payments, as observed in your test, is aligned with the example included in the spec for Automatic Payments v2.2.0-rc.2, `R/2025-09-23/P1M`, which has 16 characters.

We didn't find any restriction in the spec limiting this field to 14 characters.

Also, the middle part of the string (2025-10-11) reflects the referenceStartDate and follows the correct date format (yyyy-MM-dd) as defined in the spec.

One possibility is that the validation on your side might be enforcing a maxLength that’s not present in the official spec, or perhaps counting characters in a way that excludes hyphens, for example.

It might be worth reviewing this validation just to make sure it’s fully aligned with the spec. Please feel free to reopen this issue and let us know if we can help with anything else.
