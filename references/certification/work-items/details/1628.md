Thanks for opening the ticket,

Both the mentioned tests are not webhook tests, therefore are not expecting to receive a webhook calls during Pooling sequence. It is also important to point out that the received webook incoming calls were from payments/v3/consents/{consentId}, whilst the pooling sequence consists of a series of GET requests to ensure that, in this case, payment status has transitioned from RCVD, ACCP or ACPD to ACSC, therefore no webhook call is necessary to complete this test module.

To run test module successfully, make sure that no webhook calls are being made to conformance suit.
