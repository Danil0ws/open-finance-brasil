Unfortunately clarifying the business definitions over how the Open Finance must be used is out of the scope of the Conformance Suite Engineering Squad so there's not much we can help you on that dimension.

This channel is aimed at raising potential issues with the Conformance Tests or the APIs that are being tested using those tests. 

From a technical standpoint, it would be possible for a user with a given CPF, to authorize data to be shared between his Personal and Business accounts, or even with any other account that exists. There's no direct binding between the Receiver Account and the Transmitter Account, and as long as the transmitter provides the consent, the receiver would be able to use that token to fetch the data without any technical restriction (GDPR might provide a restriction here).

That being said, we cannot confirm if this would be allowed by the Open Finance Regulation or the UX guidelines, so I suggest you check first the UX guidelines and then, open a ticket on the service desk if the question persists.

https://openfinancebrasil.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/OF/pages/17378535/Guia+de+Experi+ncia+do+Usu+rio
