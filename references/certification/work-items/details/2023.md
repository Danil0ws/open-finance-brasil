Hi, @deboravb

After our investigation, we found that the call to the risk signals endpoint was made while the enrollment status was not `AWAITING_RISK_SIGNALS`, which correctly resulted in a 422 `STATUS_VINCULO_INVALIDO`. Below is the specification reference for this rule:

![image](/uploads/538ef203eac6fa386159d708174a4991/image.png)

Given this, we recommend retrying the test, ensuring that when calling the risk signals endpoint, the enrollment status is `AWAITING_RISK_SIGNALS`.

Feel free to open a new issue if you encounter any other problems. Happy to help!
