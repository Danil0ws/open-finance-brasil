Hello,

It's completely valid for the QRES to be sent without the amount field as it's a non-mandatory field according to the PIX manual https://www.bcb.gov.br/estabilidadefinanceira/pix?modalAberto=regulamentacao_pix.

You have to make sure that when sent, the amount on the QRCode matches the amount field on the Open Banking API. Nonetheless, if field is not sent you can just infer that the amount sent on the body of the API is valid.

In this case I also note that this is one of the objectives of the test - Make sure a QRcode without a set amount is also accepted.
