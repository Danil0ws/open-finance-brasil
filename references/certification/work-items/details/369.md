## 1. What Happened and what were you expecting?


#### discounted-credit-rights-api-test
- settlementDate in a non-mandatory field. Yet, is being required by the test.
- contractOutstandingBalance is a string field. However, the test is validating it as it is a double field.
- ballonPayments is a non-mandatory array, allowing it to not be present on the payload when the contract doesn’t have this information. However, it is being validated as required.

![discounted-credit-rights-api-test](/uploads/1200352fb79f481b46155da5a6a45678/discounted-failures.JPG)


#### discounted-credit-rights-wrong-permissions-test

POST consent is sending only customers permissions and expecting the response to be 200. However, these are not the correct permissions for discounted credit rights contracts, causing the test to fail.

How it is: 

`{"data":{"permissions":["CUSTOMERS_PERSONAL_IDENTIFICATIONS_READ","CUSTOMERS_PERSONAL_IDENTIFICATIONS_READ","RESOURCES_READ"] ...`

How it supposed to be:

`{"data":{"permissions"["LOANS_READ","LOANS_WARRANTIES_READ","LOANS_SCHEDULED_INSTALMENTS_READ","LOANS_PAYMENTS_READ","FINANCINGS_READ","FINANCINGS_WARRANTIES_READ","FINANCINGS_SCHEDULED_INSTALMENTS_READ","FINANCINGS_PAYMENTS_READ","UNARRANGED_ACCOUNTS_OVERDRAFT_READ","UNARRANGED_ACCOUNTS_OVERDRAFT_WARRANTIES_READ","UNARRANGED_ACCOUNTS_OVERDRAFT_SCHEDULED_INSTALMENTS_READ","UNARRANGED_ACCOUNTS_OVERDRAFT_PAYMENTS_READ","INVOICE_FINANCINGS_READ","INVOICE_FINANCINGS_WARRANTIES_READ","INVOICE_FINANCINGS_SCHEDULED_INSTALMENTS_READ","INVOICE_FINANCINGS_PAYMENTS_READ","RESOURCES_READ"] ... `


[API Invoice-financings - Specification](https://openbanking-brasil.github.io/openapi/swagger-apis/invoice-financings/2.0.1.yml)
