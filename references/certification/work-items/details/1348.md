Thanks for opening the ticket!

Conformance suit expects a 400 because it attempts to use a refresh token on a consumed consent.

As described in the specification

> Não deve emitir refresh_token quando o consentimento estiver no status “CONSUMED

Therefore conformance suit is operating as expected. Feel free to open a ticket if you have any other questions
