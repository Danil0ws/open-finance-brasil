## 📝 Descrição

## 1. What Happened and what were you expecting?
CS, in conformance to the specs, validates wrongly the field `remunerationTransactionRate` at Phase 4b Transactions and Transactions Current endpoints. Swagger mentions `maxLength` being 8 and does not mention any `minLength` but the regex wrongly validates that the field has exactly 8 characters:

![image](/uploads/5222937129c53e7a5ed80cf0e0f1cbc7/image.png)

CS behaviour:

![image](/uploads/e5aca526c5047c2be5b3815ef02d8cf1/image.png)

The field appears with the wrong regex in the following specs (both rc2.0 and rc3.0):
- Credit Fixed Incomes: https://openbanking-brasil.github.io/openapi/swagger-apis/credit-fixed-incomes/1.0.0-rc3.0.yml
- Bank Fixed Incomes: https://openbanking-brasil.github.io/openapi/swagger-apis/bank-fixed-incomes/1.0.0-rc3.0.yml
- Títulos do Tesouro Direto: https://openbanking-brasil.github.io/openapi/swagger-apis/treasure-titles/1.0.0-rc3.0.yml

### Comentário 1
**Autor**: Carla Moreno (@carla-raidiam)
**Criado em**: 31/07/2023, 10:36:42

The test is correct, it should follow the pattern defined in the swagger.

- For the Credit Fixed Income API, the `remunerationTransactionRate` and `indexerPercentage` fields have no `minlength` defined, GT will set the `minlenght` = 8.
- For the Fixed Income Banking API, the `remunerationTransactionRate` and `indexerPercentage` fields will have their `minlength` changed from 3 to 8.
- For the Treasury Direct Securities API, the `remunerationTransactionRate` field does not have a defined `minlength`, GT will set the `minlength` = 8.

Additionally, GT will do a general review of the APIs and check if these cases apply to other fields and make the adjustment.
