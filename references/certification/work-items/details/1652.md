Hi,

Thanks for opening the ticket, 

According to the \[DC\] Consentimento - v3.0.0 Swagger, available at: https://openbanking-brasil.github.io/openapi/swagger-apis/consents/3.0.0.yml

```
  '/consents/{consentId}/extends':
    post:
      security:
        - OAuth2AuthorizationCode:
          - openid
          - 'consent:consentId'
```

The scopes that are necessary yo execute the POST/consents/{consentId}/extends call are:

* openid 
* consent:consentId

Therefore conformance suit is operating as expected in this regard.
