Hi,

The conformance suit expects that:

* The **QRDN is valid**
* All creditor details in the QR code **exactly match** the ones configured in the test execution fields
* The payment is accepted by SPI and reaches **ACSC**

The error `DS04 – REJECTED_BY_CREDITOR_BANK` indicates that the rejection is happening, typically due to:

* Mismatch between QR code creditor data and configured creditor account
* Invalid or altered BR Code content
* Amount or remittance information inconsistency
* QR code invalid/expired at SPI level

We remain available for further clarification.

Daniela Muller
