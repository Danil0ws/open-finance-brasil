Hi Cesar! Thanks for the ticket!

The field recurringConsentId is optional, and only needed for authorisationFlow as FIDO_FLOW:

![image.png](/uploads/8c59138e303eaaff145d4fc953ac04bf/image.png)

In this case, as we are not sending this field, the option considered should be HYBRID_FLOW:

![image.png](/uploads/4f6ca73cf527bb41aea1159c6c5e7d27/image.png)

In that sense, a request without the recurringConsentID should be accepted.

We are happy to help with any additional needs!
