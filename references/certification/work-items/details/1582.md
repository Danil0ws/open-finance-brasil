After a call, we believe that there is an clarification within the Payments API specifications that needs to be addressed by the Working Group:

**Context:**\
The Payments APIs, both Payment Initiation V4 and Automatic Payments V1, includes two fields—`cnpjInitiator` and `endToEndId`—that utilize the CNPJ number from the initiating party. Additionally, the MTLS certificates (BRCACs) in use also feature the CNPJ of the certificate-holding legal entity, as detailed at [Open Finance Brasil Certificates Standard 2.1](https://openfinancebrasil.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/OF/pages/246054913/EN+Padr+o+de+Certificados+Open+Finance+Brasil+2.1).

**Problem:**\
Despite these specifications, there is no explicit requirement stating that the CNPJ numbers in the `cnpjInitiator` or `endToEndId` fields must match the CNPJ registered on the MTLS certificates. This has led to discrepancies within the ecosystem. Currently, the Conformance Suite uses a mocked CNPJ for testing these fields, whereas the MTLS certificates are provided by the institution undergoing testing. This discrepancy has caused issues; only one institution reported being blocked due to this mismatch and had to remove this validation from their sandbox environment to pass the tests, meaning that some institutions are making this validation where others not.

Given this situation, we believe it is necessary to clearly define whether these fields should be validated for consistency with the MTLS certificates. This clarity would allow us to introduce new tests to standardize practices within the ecosystem.

**Question for the WG to Evaluate:**

- Should the CNPJ detailed in the `cnpjInitiator` and `endToEndId` fields match the one registered on the MTLS certificates?
- If so, is this stipulated anywhere in the existing specifications?

Hi,

Thanks for opening the ticket!

The main purpose of the Automatic payments api as described in the specification:

> The API aims to collect consent and initiate payments between banks and financial institutions and is also accessible to commercial establishments participating in Open Finance Brasil. The resources are available to payers who are registered with an institution holding an account participating in Open Finance, regardless of whether they are individuals or companies.

Therefore a POST pix/recurring-payments is the mean in which a initiator (44771172000119) creates a payment initiation for a payer who has an account with the account holder institution, which in this case would be Banco do Brasil (00000000000191).

Taking into consideration that the conformance suit plays the initiator Role in this scenario, and Banco do Brasil is the the account holder, as described in the spec, the cnpjInitiator field is filled with, "the Payment Initiator CNPJ which is authorized to provide the Pix Initiation Service".
