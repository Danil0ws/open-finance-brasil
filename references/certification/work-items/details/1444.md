## 1. What happened and what were you expecting?

The expected outcome for the certificate verification process is:

- The Issuer of the first certificate should match the Subject name of the subsequent certificate to establish a trusted certification path.
- The certification chain should correctly reflect the hierarchy from the root CA to the participant’s certificate without any role reversals, ensuring that each certificate is correctly issued and validated.
These discrepancies are causing validation failures which are preventing our system from fully trusting the API endpoints, leading to connection issues with the affected services.
