## 1. What Happened and what were you expecting?
The jwt sended in PATCH is with aud `https://openbanking-br-api-h.mercadopago.com/open-banking/payments/v2/pix/payments`

According to the specifications the aud sended by the initiator should match the endpoint being called, in this case we assume that the aud should have the `payment_id` like `https://openbanking-br-api-h.mercadopago.com/open-banking/payments/v2/pix/payments/{payment_id}`, do you have other understanding?

And because of this divergence we are returning an error saying that the aud is invalid
