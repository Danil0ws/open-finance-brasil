## 📝 Descrição

#### Current Behavior:

---

We understand that the periodicity (`automatic.interval`) can be **SEMANAL, MENSAL, TRIMESTRAL, SEMESTRAL e ANUAL**.\
How should each be controlled? By the number of days, respectively: 7, 30, 90, 180, and 365?\
Should months with fewer or more than 30 days be considered?\
If so, what happens if, due to the adjustment, it overlaps with the start of a new payment cycle?\
Is the initial term for counting each of these periods based on the date of the actual settlement of the first payment of that recurrence (between the options of immediate payment OR the first payment actually made for that recurrence) or the date provided in the `referenceStartDateTime` field, which represents the expected date of the first payment?
