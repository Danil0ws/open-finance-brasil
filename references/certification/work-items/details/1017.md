## 📝 Descrição

## 1. What happened and what were you expecting?
The CreditCardAccountsTransactionBillValidatorV2n is incorrectly requiring the object `chargeIdentificator` as mandatory, however the cited field should only be mandatory when `paymentType` is `A_PRAZO`. Below, it follows the error and the expected behaviour, mentioned in the swagger:
