Hi Daniel! Thanks for the Response!

This is happening because, following what is described at the [swagger](https://openfinancebrasil.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/OF/pages/347079010/Informa+es+T+cnicas+-+SV+Pagamentos+-+v4.0.0) seen below, the E2EID is created in UTC, where the date of the payment is in local time. In that sense, it is necessary that your server is configured to convert these times, in order to not  refuse a payment that its correct, because of the different time zones here

![image.png](/uploads/98ada0324f0344b6c8ad2bf23dbdc814/image.png)

This test was created because institutions were complaining about this some time, and thats why this scenario was proposed. 

We are happy to help with any additional needs!
