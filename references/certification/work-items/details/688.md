## 📝 Descrição

## 1. What Happened and what were you expecting?

The field `updateIndex` at `data.defferalPeriod` and `data.grantPeriodBenefit` are incorrectly validated, causing the test to fail. Their maxLength is limited to 5 characters but in the enum list there is the type `NAO_SE_APLICA`, which by itself exceeds the given field maximum length. The cited field should consider the dictionary to define it's `maxLength`.
