## 1\. What happened and what were you expecting?

As stated in the implementation guide, when the resource is pending authorisation the correct syntax when a call is made to the resource url is 403, code: "STATUS_RESOURCE\_**PENDING_AUTHORISATION**" .

The test is expecting "STATUS_RESOURCE\_**AWAITING_AUTHORIZATION**".
