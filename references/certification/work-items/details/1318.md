## 📝 Descrição

## 1\. What happened and what were you expecting?

The Payments V4 API Specification currently supports consent for daily payments up to 2 years. During conformance testing, a scenario is tested where 729 payments are scheduled at payments_api_recurring-payments-large-batch_test-module_v4. However, only 2 institutions have successfully passed this test up to this moment.

The primary concern is that participants are facing difficulties due to a 413 error returned from their endpoint when attempting this test. As the deadline for achieving 100% compliance with the Payments V4 specifications nears, we want to highlight potential risks:

1. Participants might not meet the certification deadline due to difficulties passing the conformance test.
2. Even if participants manage to pass the test, the scenario tested does not seem feasible in a production environment. This could lead to errors and negatively impact the user experience (UX) when making payments through Open Finance.
