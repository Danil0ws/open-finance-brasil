Thanks for opening up the Ticket!

When Calling recurring consents POST endpoint with expirationDateTime as D-360, the expected response is a 422 DATA_PAGAMENTO_INVALIDA. More specifically this request calls the POST endpoint with a expirationDateTime in the past, `D` meaning the current date `-` as for a minus and `360` as for 360 days in the past.

Feel free to open another ticket if you get any other issue!
