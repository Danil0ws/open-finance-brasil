## 📝 Descrição

## 1\. What Happened and what were you expecting?
`OpfPaginationValidator` fails when trying to validate the `totalRecords` for the `/contracts/{contractId}/scheduled-instalments` endpoint. This problem arises specifically when the default parameter `page-size=25` is included in 'data.links.self' object at the response. This behavior is observed in the Phase 2 APIs listed below:

* Loans
* Financings
* Invoice-financings
* Unarranged-accounts-overdraft
