## 📝 Descrição

#### Current Behavior:
---
In `automatic-payments_api_automatic-pix-failed-firstPayment_test-module_v2` a POST recurring-payments is made expecting either a 201 or a 422. However, when a 422 is returned the test fails as it incorrectly validates that the response is a 201:

![image](/uploads/fe4177679dd8d9c0e82353b8fe588fd9/image.png){width=500}

#### Expected Behavior:
---
If a 422 is returned in the POST recurring-payments the test is expected to validate it accordingly and then proceed to the next payment attempt described in the summary.
