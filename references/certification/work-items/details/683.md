## 1. What Happened and what were you expecting?

Conformance suite is not sending header x-fapi-interaction-id in payments requests.
According to [Informa #310](https://mailchi.mp/780543062934/open-banking-informa-9291225?e=%5BUNIQID%5D) this header is mandatory from today (05/17).
