## 📝 Descrição

## 1. What happened and what were you expecting?

Conformance Suite is sending the value of the "riskSignals.automatic.lastLoginDateTime" field with the millisecond part in the date. According to the specification, the value should match the regex "^(\d{4})-(1[0-2]|0?[1-9])-(3[01]|[12][0-9]|0?[1-9])T(?:[01]\d|2[0123]):(?:[012345]\d):(?:[012345]\d)Z$".
