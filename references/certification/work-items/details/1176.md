Hi Marcelino,

Thank you for the ticket!

Regarding automatic payments, the only available feature so far is Sweeping Accounts. This product allows payments only between accounts with the same owners, as no redirection is required. For personal products, payments are possible solely between accounts sharing the exact same CPF number. In the case of CNPJ, companies within the same conglomerate are permitted to make payments among themselves, provided they share the same "root." This means if the first 8 digits of the CNPJ match, the creditors can be different for CNPJ transactions.

Currently, it's not feasible to include both CPFs and CNPJs in the array. This is because individuals can only execute payments for themselves, and companies can make payments only within their conglomerate.

We're here to assist with any additional needs you may have!
