## 1. What Happened and what were you expecting?

The value generated by the Engine for the TAG "cnpjInitiator": "02872369000110" (02.872.369/0001-10 - Hyst Publicidade & Propaganda LTDA), does not belong to the PIX BACEN ecosystem, that is, it is not a participant in the PIX BACEN and with this is why we are receiving error code 422 with the message "CNPJ_INITIATOR_INVALIDO" and therefore the test is being interrupted, making it impossible to continue the proposed scenario in the Engine. Evidence is attached in a WORD document. We await return.
