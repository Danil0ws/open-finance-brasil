We analyzed the logs of the test in question, specifically after the request `credit-cards-accounts/v2/accounts/83f3834ae2d2f4296defeeb8d07fdf7a`. We identified that your API is returning a `response_status_code` **`404 NOT_FOUND`** with `response_status_text` "Not Found".

The `404 NOT_FOUND` error indicates that the requested resource (in this case, the bill for the specified `accountId` and period) was not found on your server.

It is important to note that, based on the logs and our analysis, **there is no indication that the conformance suite is requiring the fields `data.productAdditionalInfo`, `data.networkAdditionalInfo`, `links.prev` and `links.next` to be mandatory**. The failure occurring is a `404` returned by your API, which prevents the Conformance Suit from proceeding with any validation of the response body, including checking the mandatory or optional nature of the fields you mentioned.

For us to investigate your question about the mandatory nature of the fields, it is fundamental that your API returns a `200 OK` response with a valid response body, even if empty or with optional fields omitted, if the resource does not exist. The `404` prevents any deeper validation.

Furthermore, we attempted to reproduce the situation based on the information provided, but we were unable to generate the error regarding the mandatory fields.

Once the `404` error is resolved and the CS is able to process your API's response, we can then analyze any potential issues related to the interpretation of field mandatory requirements.
