## 📝 Descrição

## 1\. What happened and what were you expecting?

The test call the POST Consents Endpoint with expirationDateTime as 2300-01-01T00:00:00Z and expects a 422 response. This behaviour is not defined on [Consents v2.2.0-rc.1 swagger](https://openfinancebrasil.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/OF/pages/196870212/Informa+es+T+cnicas+-+Consentimento+-+v2.2.0-rc.1), as seen below:

![image](/uploads/b3c2a2ee83ab679ffc0c0cd6675ea8b4/image.png){width="579" height="160"}

As this is a payload malformed syntax, we believe the response should be 400 BAD REQUEST as this is the standard response when a field fail a syntax validation.

In this specific case, the behavior being enforced is that we a consent with infinite term cannot be created, although an extension can be executed on this matter. In that sense, the syntax of it its correct, but is should be banned by the business rule mentioned above, and thats why we are expecting a 422.

We understand that the specification does not make it clear this statement, so we will raise this to the working groups in order to address this on the next versions. By all mean, the test behavior does not need adjustments, since this is the behavior that should be enforced.
