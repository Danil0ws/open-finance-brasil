## 📝 Descrição

## 1. What happened and what were you expecting?

After sending the risk signals, the enrollment is in **AWAITING_ACCOUNT_HOLDER_VALIDATION** status. After 15 minutes, the engine expects a _rejectionReason_ **REJEITADO_TEMPO_EXPIRADO_ENROLLMENT** but in the technical specification for this scenario it would be **REJEITADO_TEMPO_EXPIRADO_ACCOUNT_HOLDER_VALIDATION**

Logs: https://web.conformance.directory.openbankingbrasil.org.br/log-detail.html?log=OFUaOY8lnv9Bqc3
