### Comentário 6
**Autor**: Matheus Mantovani (@matheus-mantovani)
**Criado em**: 15/01/2026, 17:46:32

Hello @guilherme.melo4. Thanks for raising this.

We reviewed the behavior with the OPF Structure Product team (DTO) and agreed that, in this scenario, the rejection may not be immediate. The Pix arrangement allows up to **40 seconds** for the transaction to be completed (successfully or rejected), as per this [document](https://www.bcb.gov.br/content/estabilidadefinanceira/pix/Regulamento_Pix/IX_ManualdeTemposdoPix.pdf), so calling GET /pix/recurring-payments/{recurringPaymentId} immediately after a 201 can be too early.

As requested by DTO we will add **polling for up to 40 seconds** after the 201 response, re-checking the payment status until it transitions to RJCT (with rejectionReason = LIMITE_VALOR_TRANSACAO_CONSENTIMENTO_EXCEDIDO) or the timeout is reached. This will make the test robust to asynchronous processing while staying aligned with the arrangement timing.

We'll also keep you posted on this update.

Best regards
