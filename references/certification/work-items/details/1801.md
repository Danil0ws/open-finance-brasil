Hello, @caio.tranjan. We are facing the following issue: we are currently only in Phase 3. However, according to the information available [here](https://openfinancebrasil.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/OF/pages/245694465/PT+Open+Finance+Brasil+Financial-grade+API+Security+Profile+1.0+Implementers+Draft+3#5.2.2.4-Escopos-obrigat%C3%B3rios-no-endpoint-de-descoberta-(well-known)), the following scopes are mandatory, regardless of whether the institution provides the related products or not:

- invoice-financings
- financings
- loans
- unarranged-accounts-overdraft
- bank-fixed-incomes
- credit-fixed-incomes
- variable-incomes
- treasure-titles
- funds
- exchanges

