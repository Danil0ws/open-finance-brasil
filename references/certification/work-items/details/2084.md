#### Current Behavior:
---

After the payment being scheduled, a cancellation by the receiver happens as following:

![image](/uploads/329d5f1b068c65ca01a1ece8a60de839/image.png){width=486 height=314}

Then it performs a `GET` on the payment and throws an error as following:

![image](/uploads/5699d9aaab56ea03e1a654f43721fd61/image.png){width=582 height=129}

#### Expected Behavior:
---

[The specification](https://openbanking-brasil.github.io/openapi/swagger-apis/automatic-payments/?urls.primaryName=2.0.0-rc.1#/Recurring%20Consents/automaticPaymentsGetRecurringConsentsConsentId) indeed says that `$.data.cancellation.cancelledBy.document.identification` must have up to 11 characters, but the defined pattern accepts a CNPJ (and the cancellation was indeed made by a CNPJ).

![image](/uploads/9ac704811920f5df7630f7eb0847c01f/image.png){width=752 height=445}

From our understanding, this attribute should accept either CPF or CNPJ, since the cancellation can be performed by both sides - but the specification is confusing. Could you confirm this behavior, please?
