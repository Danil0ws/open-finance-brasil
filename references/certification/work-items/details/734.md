## 1. What Happened and what were you expecting?

In `dcr_api_dcm-pagto-remove-webhook_test-module` there are two different POST payments happening with the same amount in a short period of time, which can be an issue to institution's PIX system as it could be wrongly categorized as a duplicated transaction.

The new expected behavior is that the 2nd POST payments amount is changed to 0.02 instead of 0.01.
