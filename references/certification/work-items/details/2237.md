### Comentário 4
**Autor**: Caio Tranjan (@caio.tranjan)
**Criado em**: 18/06/2025, 18:04:51

Hi,

Thanks for opening the issue!

The Conformance Suite is currently aligned with the Swagger specification and is functioning as expected. As such, no changes are required on the suite side at this time.

We believe the points you've raised pertain to potential issues within the specification itself, and therefore recommend they be evaluated by the relevant Working Group.

Specifically, your observations on the `GET /contract/{contractId}/payments` endpoint are noted as follows:

* **`lastUpdatedcontractOutstandingBalanceDateTime`**: The term “contract” is in lowercase, which does not conform to the expected camelCase pattern.
* **`paidInstalments`**: You correctly noted the spelling with a single “l”; we’d appreciate clarification on whether the standard should adopt British or American English conventions.
* **`lastUpdatedcontractOutstandingBalanceDateTime`**: The defined `maxLength` is 20, which does not account for milliseconds. Participant requested a change in the specification to contemplate milliseconds .

We appreciate your attention to detail and recommend forwarding these observations to the WG for further consideration.
