## 1. What Happened and what were you expecting? - 

From Service Desk 11919

> Test Name:credit-cards-api-operational-limits
> Variant:client_auth_type=mtls, fapi_auth_request_method=by_value, fapi_profile=openbanking_brazil, fapi_response_mode=plain_response
> Test ID:SsLZ3Dsl3u9WLug
> Created:Tue Oct 18 2022 10:14:28 GMT-0300 (Brasilia Standard Time)
> Description:Functional test for Mercado Pago's OpenBanking - Operational Limits
> Test Version:4.1.43 build: 20b47b07264f23c6630d52164600039a4a4f412f
> Plan ID:xYqKwAf4D63cC
> 
> Dear, we are having a problem running the above test.
> 
> - The problem seems to be caused by the Access Token expiration, which takes 15 mins, and the test takes more than 15 mins to run in full.
> 
> Let's detail a test as an example: [https://web.conformance.directory.openbankingbrasil.org.br/log-detail.html?log=SsLZ3Dsl3u9WLug&public=true](https://web.conformance.directory.openbankingbrasil.org .br/log-detail.html?log=SsLZ3Dsl3u9WLug&public=true)
> 
> 1 - At 10:14:49 an Access Token is returned with an expiration time of 899 seconds.
> 
> 2 - At 10:17:44 a new Access Token is generated in order to update the Token, also with an expiration time of 899 seconds.
> 
> 3 - Looking inside this last generated Token, we notice that the 'exp' field mentions that the expiration will take place at 10:32:53.
> 
> 4 - At 10:33:46, we observed that the response of the CallProtectedResource step returned a 401 UNAUTHORISED status, whereas the expected was a 200.
> 
> 5 - The test is interrupted by failure.
> 
> - We draw attention to the fact that in step #3 of the explanation, the expiration would be at 10:32:53, but according to step #4 of the explanation, there was a call to the api /credit-cards-accounts/v2 /accounts/168784141/limits after this time, at 10:33:46, which generates the 401 error.
> 
> - Access Token expires in 15 mins, and was only refreshed once during the test, more than 15 mins ago, and the test takes more than 15 mins to finish its execution, and because of that our API responds with a 401 , interrupting the test.
> 
> - We believe that this may be a problem in the engine's strategy in this test, as it could ensure that the token is not expired, before making a new call.


## 2. What should be done

Either the refresh token sequence must be called in between less API calls or some logic needs to be set to call a refresh token X minutes before it gets to a point where it will expire
