## 📝 Descrição

## 1. What happened and what were you expecting?

Considerations about some v3 tests:

payments_api_multiple-consents-no-funds-conditional_test-module_v3
payments_api_qrdn-good-proxy_test-module_v3
payments_api_qrdn-with-qres-code_test-module_v3

If compared to v2, these tests had considerable changes. Some business rules, wich were previously (on v2) validated on payment flow, now they have to run on consent flow (on v3). Our understanding is that v3 has role conflicts between these flows. The Security layer can't validate business roles like account balance or qrdn validations. The security layer roles should be limited to guaranteeing the consent lifecycle, as its authentication and authorization.

Facing the exposed, we request to review the solution architecture of v3 in order to reestablish the roles of security and business layers.
