## 📝 Descrição

## 1. What Happened and what were you expecting?

The usage of a date field instead of DateTime field has already brought problems on the last phase as it was unclear to institutions if they had to use either UTC or UTC-3 as their reference time.

In order to avoid these issues with the new version of the APIs we recommend setting this field as a DateTime or allowing at least to have the Z which denotes the Greenwich (UTC) time zone, avoiding ambiguity

Note that this has already brought a few issues on Gitlab:
https://gitlab.com/obb1/certification/-/issues/65
https://gitlab.com/obb1/certification/-/issues/74

The Well Known problem is the following 
![image](/uploads/e9bd75ce83454e1c4b2a7235e243712c/image.png)

## 2. Proposed Solution

Either make both fields as DateTime or Update the Date field to also receive a Z at its end going from

2021-05-21 to 2021-05-21Z
