#### Current Behavior:

---

O teste automatic-payments_api_automatic-pix-wrong-creditor_test-module_v2n2 está falhando porque, após o envio do POST recurring-payments com conta crédito inválida de outra instituição (ispb de teste do bacen), o motor faz o _polling_ enquanto o status retornado for RCVD ou ACCP. Como o pix é enviado para outra instituição, antes desta enviar a resposta rejeitando a transação, o status do pix em nossa base de dados passa para ACPD. Dessa forma o motor recebe ACPD no GET e sai do loop pois é diferente de RCVD e de ACCP.

#### Expected Behavior:

---

Como o cenário de teste prevê um ispb de crédito externo, espera-se que o motor fique em loop aguardando por um status diferente de RCVD, de ACCP e também de ACPD.  Se não tratar o ACPD como status para manter o polling, nunca vai receber o RJCT pois o motor falha antes

Hi WG,

Participant is reporting this situation:

_The automatic-payments_api_automatic-pix-wrong-creditor_test-module_v2n2 test is failing because, after sending the recurring-payments POST with an invalid credit account from another institution (Bacen test ispb), the CS polls while the returned status is RCVD or ACCP._

_As the test scenario provides for an external credit ispb, the CS is expected to loop while waiting for a status other than RCVD, ACCP, and also ACPD. If ACPD is not treated as a status to maintain polling, RJCT will never be received because the CS fails before_

The test does not accept the ACPD status in polling:

• Poll the GET recurring-payments {recurringPaymentId} while the Status is RCVD or ACCP

The participant requested a review of the test behaviour: the reported problem is in sending an immediate, unscheduled payment, as described in the image attached to this response if it were a scheduled payment, the status would remain SCHD until we received a rejection from the SPI (since the credit ISPB is from another institution). So the test would have to loop until it was effective the next day (if it was the first payment) or at least two days of looping (if it was a scheduled immediate payment).

Could you please verify?

Hi,

This situation was agreed upon at the request of the Open Finance structure in the issue: https://gitlab.com/raidiam-conformance/open-finance/certification/-/issues/2028

At this pooling sequence **ACPD** is not a possible state for the payment as it is achieved only after **SCHD** state. So payment can not reach SCHD after reached ACPD.

Please, feel free to reach out in case of further questions!
