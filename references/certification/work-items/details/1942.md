### Comentário 14
**Autor**: Carla Moreno (@carla-raidiam)
**Criado em**: 03/01/2025, 23:44:11

Hi,

Thanks for the ticket!

We understand your question and believe it is indeed pertinent. After analyzing the provided log, we noticed the enum list returned by CS aligns exactly with the specification. However, this revealed a potential inconsistency in the specification:

- The enum `DETALHE_PAGAMENTO_INVALIDO` is currently only available for a 422 response to POST recurring-payments. However, we believe in this scenario the error could also be returned asynchronously, as your implementation is doing. Therefore, clarification from the WG is needed:
  - Shouldn't `DETALHE_PAGAMENTO_INVALIDO` also be included as a valid enum for the field `data.rejectionReason` in the 201 response to POST recurring-payments and in the 200 response to GET recurring-payments? 
    - If this enum is added in a future version of the API, what behavior should CS adopt in the meantime?

@ivanzb FYI: meanwhile, we want to highlight that this does not block the test from being successfully completed. Returning 422 `DETALHE_PAGAMENTO_INVALIDO` in the POST recurring-payments test step will ensure a smooth test execution and avoid potential disruptions related to this specification issue.

Let us know if you need further clarification. Happy to help!
