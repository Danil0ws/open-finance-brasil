HI Joao! Accordgin to https://openfinancebrasil.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/OF/pages/245694465/PT+Open+Finance+Brasil+Financial-grade+API+Security+Profile+1.0+Implementers+Draft+3#5.2.2.4-Escopos-obrigat%C3%B3rios-no-endpoint-de-descoberta-(well-known):

O servidor de autorização deve obrigatoriamente declarar os escopos abaixo em seu endpoint de descoberta (well-known), independentemente se a instituição forneça ou não os produtos referentes aos escopos abaixo listados:

* invoice-financings
* financings
* loans
* unarranged-accounts-overdraft
* bank-fixed-incomes
* credit-fixed-incomes
* variable-incomes
* treasure-titles
* funds
* exchanges

In that sense, follwoing whst is defined at the security spec, the well known should declare these scopes even if they are providade or not. We are happy to help with any additional needs!
