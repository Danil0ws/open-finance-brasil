**Autor**: Daniela Muller (@danielamllr)
**Criado em**: 08/05/2025, 17:16:57

Hi @gimendes 

After analyzing the internal logs, we identified that the root cause of the failure was not the final payment status itself, but rather a timeout error during the polling sequence.

The specific error returned by the test engine was: `Participant took too long to reject request, failing.`

This indicates that the participant's server took an excessively long time to update the payment status during the polling process. The test has a time limit to wait for these status updates, and this limit was reached before the final status was achieved, leading to the test being interrupted.

To improve the clarity and robustness of this test, we will open a test improvement ticket with the following points:

- Extend the polling timeout: This will allow more time for servers with potential latencies to update the payment status, reducing the occurrence of premature timeouts.
- Adjust the failure message: The current message `Participant took too long to reject request` will be changed to more accurately indicate the cause of the failure (polling timeout).

If you have any further questions, please do not hesitate to contact us.
