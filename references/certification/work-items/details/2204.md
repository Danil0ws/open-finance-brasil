Hi,

We would like to clarify an important point about the nature of this test. The objective of this specific test is to validate the system's behavior when handling a revoked certificate, and not an expired certificate.

A revoked certificate is one that, although it may have a valid expiration date in the future, has been explicitly invalidated before its expiration date. This revocation can occur for various reasons, such as private key compromise.

The test in question uses a certificate that was generated and subsequently revoked by our infrastructure specifically to simulate this scenario. Therefore, even if the certificate has a valid expiration date in the future, the focus of the test is to verify whether the server correctly checks the revocation status of the certificate (likely through OCSP - Online Certificate Status Protocol requests) and prevents access when the certificate is revoked.

Thank you for your attention to detail, and we hope this explanation helps clarify the purpose of this specific test. If you have any further questions, please do not hesitate to inform us.
