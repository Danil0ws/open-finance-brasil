Hi Marcelino! Thanks for the ticket!

Although compliant with the regex, the iss being sent by the CS is a random value that does not correspond to the organisationID, as stated at [phase 3v2 swagger](https://openfinancebrasil.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/OF/pages/24182940/Como+Assinar+o+Payload+-+v2.0.0+-+Pagamentos):

(requisição JWT e resposta JWT): o receptor da mensagem deverá validar se o valor do campo iss coincide com o organisationId da outra parte listado no diretório

In that sense, the validation that should take place is if the iss is the organisationID, and not only if it is a compliant value.
