## 📝 Descrição

Good afternoon,

The field transactionUnitPrice is marked as mandatory, and its description has been updated to indicate exclusive use for "custody transfers."

What is the expected behavior for this field when the transactionType is different from "custody transfers"? Our understanding is that, in such cases, the field should not be mandatory. Instead, it should have a conditional requirement that enforces its inclusion only when the transactionType is set to "custody transfer."

We look forward to your clarification.
