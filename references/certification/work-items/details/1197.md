## 1\. What happened and what were you expecting?

There are two tests that validate business person creditors. Both are sending random CNPJ for the creditors that are different from our informed businessEntity CNPJ in the test configuration plan.

According to issue #1176 we are supposed to validate if the all the creditors root of CNPJs (the first 8 numbers) are the same as the root of the bussinessEntity CNPJ. In the **automatic-payments_api_sweeping-accounts-invalid-creditor_test-module_v1** test it correctly expects an error, but on the **automatic-payments_api_sweeping-accounts-root-cnpj_test-module_v1** test it expects the payload to succeed.

So what should be done? If I validate the CNPJ root, the **automatic-payments_api_sweeping-accounts-invalid-creditor_test-module_v1** test will succeed and the **automatic-payments_api_sweeping-accounts-root-cnpj_test-module_v**1 test will fail, and if I don't validate, the opposite will occur.

Hi Francielle,

Thank you for the ticket! The validation executed in both tests ensures that a consent or payment can be carried out only between companies sharing the same root. Specifically, the validations are as follows:

1. If the first 8 digits of the CNPJs in the creditor are identical, then consent is granted, and payment can be executed.
2. If the CNPJs have different roots, then consent cannot be granted.

In this context, the "automatic-payments_api_sweeping-accounts-root-cnpj_test-module_v1" validates the first scenario, where the CNPJs share the same root. Conversely, the "automatic-payments_api_sweeping-accounts-invalid-creditor_test-module_v1" validates the second scenario, where they have different roots.
