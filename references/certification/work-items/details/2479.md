## 📝 Descrição

In the enrollments_api_automatic-payment_sign-options-invalid-permission_test-module_v2-2 scenario, during fido-sign-options, the recurring consent is being passed as consentId instead of recurringConsentId, which is the expected parameter. In other words, although the scenario aims to test that sending a recurringConsent is not allowed when the corresponding permission is missing in the enrollment, the parameter provided should be the correct one to ensure the validity of the test.
