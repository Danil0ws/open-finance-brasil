## 📝 Descrição

## 1. What happened and what were you expecting?

When trying to cancel the remaining payments associated with a consent, the test-module is sending a request to the url `PATCH /open-banking/payments/v4/consents/<consent-id>`.

This route does not exist, the correct endpoint in the [specification](https://openfinancebrasil.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/OF/pages/264307138/Informa+es+T+cnicas+-+SV+Pagamentos+-+v4.0.0-rc.1) is in fact `/pix/payments/consents/{consentId}`, so the request should have been made to `PATCH /open-banking/payments/v4/pix/payments/consents/<consent-id>`
