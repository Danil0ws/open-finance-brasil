## 📝 Descrição

## 1\. What Happened and what were you expecting?

As seen at https://gitlab.com/raidiam-conformance/open-finance/certification/-/issues/820, the resources_api_unavailable_test-module_v2 is also duplicating th resource on the redirect URL:

![image.png](/uploads/bf213a1c9953f8f236d6ee15422d2023/image.png)

Although according to the [security specs](https://openfinancebrasil.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/OF/pages/82083996/EN+Open+Finance+Brasil+Financial-grade+API+Security+Profile+1.0+Implementers+Draft+3#7.2.2.-Authorization-server), the Authorization Server:

5. shall not reject an authorisation request requesting _scopes_ broader than those necessary to access data specified in the Permissions element of a linked Consent Resource object;

In that sense, since the conformance suite is sending the correct scope twice and the scope is correct, the token issued shouldn't have this problem, even though the scope is duplicated.

By all means, as this test is not intended to test this behavior, we should fix.
