`kid: h6F107y0ncJj_6JQNQ-6nauLBlT1IgmMig8CeAt94U4`

`https://keystore.sandbox.directory.openbankingbrasil.org.br/96748716-9b96-448c-a2b7-2abf9762af8e/44db0e03-d7fb-478e-9573-a66e254c587a/application.jwks`
