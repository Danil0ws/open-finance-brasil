## 📝 Descrição

## 1\. What Happened and what were you expecting?

`ValidateNumberOfRecordsPage1` and `OpfPaginationValidator` fail when executing a GET call to transactions or transactions-current endpoints without the page=1 parameter or page-size=25. However, CS should accept the returning of a self link without the page or page-size parameters as they are optional and the default values are 1 and 25 respectively. Currently, the following errors occur:

- On `OpfPaginationValidator` step at phase 4b test modules:
