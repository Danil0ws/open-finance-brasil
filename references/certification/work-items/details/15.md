## 📝 Descrição

While running the functional tests for registration data API, I've found a divergent field name:

- In the [Open Banking Brasil portal](https://openbanking-brasil.github.io/areadesenvolvedor/#tocS_PersonalQualificationData) (v1.0.0-rc7.2) we have the procurator's document field called _cpfNumber_.
- In the [data directory](https://openbanking-brasil.github.io/areadesenvolvedor/dictionary/customersGetPersonalFinancialRelations_v1.csv), we also have this field called _cpfNumber_.

However...

- In the [swagger](https://openbanking-brasil.github.io/areadesenvolvedor/swagger/swagger_customers_apis.yaml), the field is called _cnpjCpfNumber_.
- In the conformance suite, the test NaturalPersonRelationshipResponseValidator.java expect this field named as _cnpjCpfNumber_.

Should I consider _cnpjCpfNumber_?
