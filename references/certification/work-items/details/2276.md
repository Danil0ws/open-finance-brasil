#### Current Behavior:
---
When the test makes a payment request with an invalid amount, the portability status is changed to the `PAYMENT_ISSUE`.  
After that, the test queries the portability on endpoint `GET /portabilities/{portabilityId}` and validates if the status is `PAYMENT_ISSUE` and if the field `statusReason.paymentIssueDetail` is present. However, this field was removed in `beta3` specification version.

#### Expected Behavior:
---
After receiving a portability with `PAYMENT_ISSUE` status, the test should no longer validate the `statusReason.paymentIssueDetail` field.
