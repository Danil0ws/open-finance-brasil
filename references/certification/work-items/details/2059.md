As indicated by the reported failure:

"code": "PAGAMENTO_DIVERGENTE_CONSENTIMENTO",
"detail": "The date of the first occurrence of a payment associated with a billing cycle must be later than the settlement date of the service membership payment. FirstPayment.Date '24/02/2025' / ReferenceStartDate '24/02/2025'."

In the POST automatic-payments/v2/pix/recurring-payments, the following values were sent:

"firstPayment": {
   "date": "2025-02-24"
},
"referenceStartDate": "2025-02-24"

**Specification Rules**
**referenceStartDate**: Represents the expected date for the first occurrence of a recurring payment.
**firstPayment.date**: Defines the target date for the settlement of the initial payment.

📌 6.1.5 - Payment and Consent Discrepancy
"Validates whether the payment data differs from the consent data (PAGAMENTO_DIVERGENTE_CONSENTIMENTO)."

The date of the first occurrence of a payment associated with a billing cycle must be **later** than the target date for settlement of the of the initial payment.
