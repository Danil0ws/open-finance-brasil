## 📝 Descrição


## 1. What Happened and what were you expecting?

During the resources-api-test-unavailable-v2 test, the API is expected to return a resource with the status TEMPORY_UNAVAILABLE/UNAVAILABLE shortly after the consent is approved. However, during the consent journey, only resources currently available on digital channels should be presented for selection (User Experience Guide - page 99 https://openbankingbrasil.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/OB/pages/6128557/ +User+Experience+Guide). Therefore, it is not possible to select these features in the case of Accounts and Credit Card Accounts.

Therefore, we believe that the test should be modified to wait a period (suggestion of 10 min polling) to allow time to modify the status of a resource that was scoped for consent, after its approval.
