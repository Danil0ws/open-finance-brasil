The test failed because your implementation is missing mandatory fields in the 201 response for POST recurring-consents:

  - `data.recurringConfiguration.sweeping.useOverdraftLimit`
  - `data.recurringConfiguration.sweeping.startDateTime `

The error is better shown below:

![image](/uploads/c41bd00cb4c0db3b46082ba34c362637/image.png){width=500}

It follows specification ([API Automatic Payments v2.0.0-beta.2](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/OpenBanking-Brasil/openapi/main/swagger-apis/automatic-payments/2.0.0-beta.2.yml)) reference:

![image](/uploads/2427df0169ec404ea5c1b1e1c9414d86/image.png){width=500}

Therefore, no corrections are needed on the CS side. To ensure successful execution, the implementation must include all mandatory fields in the responses.

Feel free to open another ticket if you get any other issue! Happy to help!
