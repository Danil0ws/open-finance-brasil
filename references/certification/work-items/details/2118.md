## 📝 Descrição

The test is flawed because it requires execution between 9 PM and 12 AM BRT while setting startDateTime as now + 1 hour. **If the test runs between 11 PM and 12 AM**, startDateTime moves to the next day, causing a mismatch and a PAGAMENTO_DIVERGENTE_CONSENTIMENTO error. **The test only succeeds between 9 PM and 11 PM.**
