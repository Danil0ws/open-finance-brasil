Thanks for the reply! We understand that the CS behavior is compliant since both the QRDN and transactionIdentification are being provided by the user. 

Regarding the specification support, as discussed at #522 and stated at the Payments Swagger:

![image](/uploads/b5690b0904ef152a158b3ab088c63e5a/image.png)

The transactionIdentification is mandatory for localInstrument as QRDN, and should be the same value as the TxId from the QRDN JSON payload.

To find the transactionIdentification, The [PIX Documentation](https://www.bcb.gov.br/content/estabilidadefinanceira/pix/Regulamento_Pix/II_ManualdePadroesparaIniciacaodoPix.pdf) states that the value should be retrieved from the JSON Payload, and not the from the QRCODE itself, which may be causing the error:

![image](/uploads/eb8b95d56ac285271cfb3c9c43abe7e0/image.png)

Happy to help with additional need!
