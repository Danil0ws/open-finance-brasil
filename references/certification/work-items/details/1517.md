## 📝 Descrição

## 1. What happened and what were you expecting?
When validating consent, we get an error response that the statusUpdateDateTime is not later than the request time.
Because the specified datetime RFC3339 used in the open finance doesn't show milliseconds differences, our endpoint process the request in less than 1 second, resulting the statusUpdatedTime of the payments equal to requestDateTime.
