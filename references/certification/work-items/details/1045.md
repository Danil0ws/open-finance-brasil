## 📝 Descrição

## 1\. What happened and what were you expecting?

#### **1. Observed Behavior:**

As seen at https://web.conformance.directory.openbankingbrasil.org.br/log-detail.html?log=bNDD1LfSUXLVw4Z, the OpfPaginationValidator is comparing the requestUrl and self link, without considering that the page-size can be different from the one requested.

As described at https://openfinancebrasil.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/OF/pages/17377470/Pagina+o#Regras-Adicionais: 

* "The transmitting/holding institution may define a maximum page size (page-size) smaller than the maximum size allowed by the API if it deems it necessary to reduce the limit to meet the response SLA."

Which means that when comparing the requestUrl with the selfLink, the page-size parameter in the request can be lower than the one requested.

#### **2. Expected Behavior:**

The OpfPaginationValidator should also accept page-size values lower than the ones requested, and accept this is a valid behavior when comparing the requestUrl and the self link.

#### **3. Evidence (test ID):**

https://web.conformance.directory.openbankingbrasil.org.br/log-detail.html?log=bNDD1LfSUXLVw4Z
