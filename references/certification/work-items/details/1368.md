Hi Caik! Sorry for taking so long to answer.

At payments_api_recurring-payments-unhappy-date-adjustment_test-module_v4, it is stated "When dealing with months that do not have a 31st, send the date as the previous day" because as the name of the module says, the CS is acting wrongly, and thats why it is expected a 422 - PAGAMENTO_DIVERGENTE_CONSENTIMENTO at this call.

On the other side,payments_api_recurring-payments-monthly-core_test-module_v4 does the correct adjustments, as "Additionally, when dealing with months that do not have a 31st, send the date as the next day, which is the 1st of the next month", thats why the payments is successful.
