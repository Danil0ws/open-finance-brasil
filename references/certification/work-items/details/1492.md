## 1. What happened and what were you expecting?
The test consents_api_negative-extensions_test-module_v3 is validating an incorrect error message. While the test expects the error message `COMBINACAO_PERMISSOES_INCORRETA`, the correct spelling is `COMBINACOES_DE_PERMISSOES_INCORRETA` according to the Open Finance documentation.

Link to doc: https://openfinancebrasil.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/OF/pages/219480491/Orienta+es+-+DC+Consentimento#Regras-para-a-cria%C3%A7%C3%A3o-do-consentimento
Link to test log: https://web.conformance.directory.openbankingbrasil.org.br/log-detail.html?log=GmqnAlAcTZ32D9z
