The test validation requiring execution between 12 PM and 11:59 PM is in place to ensure that the test simulates a scenario where an intraday retry occurs in the correct timeframe, without conflicting with other retry rules.

According to the Intraday Retry Window, the second attempt for intraday processing happens between 6 PM and 9 PM (UTC-3), as outlined in the specification. However, the test execution window extends from 12 PM onwards to ensure:

**Controlled Testing Conditions:**

Running the test after 12 PM UTC-3 guarantees that the second intraday retry falls within the expected 6 PM - 9 PM timeframe.

**Prevention of Early Execution:**

If executed earlier than 12 PM, the system might still be within the first intraday execution window (0h - 8h UTC-3), making the test invalid.

**Conclusion:**

The validation ensures that the test only runs in a time frame that allows the correct sequence of events, simulating the second intraday attempt without interference from the first attempt rules.
