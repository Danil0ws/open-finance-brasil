## 📝 Descrição

## 1\. What happened and what were you expecting?

During GET call of module automatic-payments_api_sweeping-accounts-consents-core_test-module_v1 suit is sending "https://obkhmo-mtls.brb.com.br/open-banking/automatic-payments/v1/recurring-consents/4CD8A3CDE66AA1BA9AFA8757D081AE0E". note that the recurring consent ID is without its prefix "urn:bank:", as expected given the specs:

"O **`recurringConsentId`** é o identificador único do consentimento de longa duração e deverá ser um URN - Uniform Resource Name.\
Um URN, conforme definido na [RFC8141](https://tools.ietf.org/html/rfc8141) é um Uniform Resource Identifier - URI - que é atribuído sob o URI scheme "urn" e um namespace URN específico, com a intenção de que o URN seja um identificador de recurso persistente e independe da localização."

Therefore, the expected URL should be "[https://obkhmo-mtls.brb.com.br/open-banking/automatic-payments/v1/recurring-consents/urn:BRB:4CD8A3CDE66AA1BA9AFA8757D081AE0E](https://obkhmo-mtls.brb.com.br/open-banking/automatic-payments/v1/recurring-consents/4CD8A3CDE66AA1BA9AFA8757D081AE0E)"
