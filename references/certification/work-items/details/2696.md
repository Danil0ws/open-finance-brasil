## 📝 Descrição

**Module:** `customer-personal_api_portability_test-module_v2-3`

**Validator:** `EnsurePortabilitiesReceived`

---

**Current behavior**

The validator fails when `portabilitiesReceived` is absent from the `GET /personal/financial-relations` response, returning:

`data.portabilitiesReceived is empty`

**Spec (OAS 2.3.0)**

`portabilitiesReceived` is **not listed** in the `required` array of `PersonalFinancialRelationData`. The field is optional. The `minItems: 1` constraint defined in the field's schema only applies if the field is present in the response — it does not imply the field is mandatory.

**Expected behavior**

Confirm with DTO/AOPF whether the expected behavior has changed.
