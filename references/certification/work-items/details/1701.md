## 📝 Descrição

#### Current Behavior:

---

The Autyomatic Payments V1 spec, at the PATCH recurring-payments/{recurringPaymentId}, there is the following cancellation reason: **CANCELADO_MULTIPLAS_ALCADAS**

![image.png](/uploads/211285b66aa53d8a43519b69dd37dd41/image.png){width=740 height=258}

But in Auitomatic Payments, as we have at Payments V4, the multiple consents is controlled by the consent with the status PARTIALLY-ACCEPTED, and the is no status in PATC. IN that sense this message is useless.

**In that sense, we would like to confirm with the WG is this shoudl not be deleted.**
