Hi Fernando! Thanks for the ticket!

The test is actually falling because the field totalRecords is being sent on the 422 response of the POST Payments API, different from what is been defined on the swagger:

![image](/uploads/41ed9a68d9d4621f8ff3588442e0711a/image.png)

Additional Properties can be sent on the payload, but they need to follow [Extensibilidade Specs](https://openfinancebrasil.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/OF/pages/17377516/Extensibilidade), and comply with the regex ^(\[A-Z\]{4})(-)(.\*)$. In this specific case, for example, the field should be sent as RAID-totalRecords, with these four initial letters as the unique identifiers from your institution.

We are happy to help with any additional needs!
