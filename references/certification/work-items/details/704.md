Hi,

On the test you have provided you have sent TPP and the test is expecting USER

![image](/uploads/9f63de22e65ac65b9eaa96cbb22cf655/image.png)

The reason we have set this is because of the Phase 2 implementation guidelines - https://openfinancebrasil.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/OF/pages/17378841/Guia+de+implementa+o+v2.0+-+Dados+cadastrais+e+transacionais

On page 58 you have the following table:

![image](/uploads/b9391edaf57343e0082b4b65715bccaa/image.png)

Unfortunately, as this is purely a business decision there's not much that the Conformance Team can do around updating or discussing this test scenario, as our focus is ensuring the usage of international standards. 

If you believe this behavior is to be updated we suggest you to open a ticket on https://servicedesk.openfinancebrasil.org.br/ direct to the specifications team
