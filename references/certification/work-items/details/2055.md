#### Current Behavior:

---

In the test scenario automatic-payments_api_automatic-pix-revoked_test-module_v2, the expected status for the payment at the end of the process is RJCT. However, we are currently returning CANC.

Considering that the revocation was performed by the initiator, the correct status should be RJCT, as stated in the documentation:

RJCT (Rejected): Indicates that the transaction was rejected by the holder or the SPI. Since the revocation was not carried out by the holder but by the initiator, it makes sense for the payment status to be RJCT instead of CANC.

As stated in the Automatic Payments v2 specifcation, inside the GET pix/recurring-payments/{recurringPaymentId} is is described:

> * CANC (Cancelled) - Indica que a transação Pix pendente foi cancelada com sucesso pelo usuário antes que fosse confirmada (ACCP) ou rejeitada (RJCT) pela detentora.

And also:

> 
>
> * rejectionReason:
>   * CONSENTIMENTO_REVOGADO: O pagamento estava associado a um consentimento que foi revogado.

In this test module, we execute the following step:

• Call the PATCH recurring-consents endpoint

Notice that it is the consent that is revoked and not the payment itself. Therefore Conformance Suit is correctly expecting the payment to be in RJCT state with rejectionReason = CONSENTIMENTO_REVOGADO, working as expected in this regard.
