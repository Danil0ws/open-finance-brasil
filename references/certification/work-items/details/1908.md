Hi @rafael.almeidaa ,

Questions around certificates should be limited to the OPF Brazil Service Desk, nonetheless please see below the clarification for your point.

The Certificate That you have provided was issued by the chain "| AC SOLUTI RFB V5 |", which is not an authorized CA as per the [Open Finance Brazil Certificate Standard](https://openfinancebrasil.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/OF/pages/246054913/EN+Padr+o+de+Certificados+Open+Finance+Brasil+2.1)

The List of Soluti Authorized CAs can be found [here](https://www.gov.br/iti/pt-br/assuntos/repositorio/ac-soluti)

Upon further research we have identified that this Certificate is found on another list - https://www.gov.br/iti/pt-br/assuntos/repositorio/ac-soluti-rfb-de-2deg-nivel which is not part of the Open Finance Certificate Types

As noted on the [Open Finance Brazil Certificate Standard](https://openfinancebrasil.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/OF/pages/246054913/EN+Padr+o+de+Certificados+Open+Finance+Brasil+2.1) "Only the certificates indicated with "Situação: válido" (which mean "status: valid") in these ITI repositories referenced above, which are Chain v5 and v10, should be accepted by the servers of the Open Finance Brasil ecosystem."

It seems that either you or Soluti made a mistake when issuing this Certificate. I would recommended checking back with Soluti around the point above.
