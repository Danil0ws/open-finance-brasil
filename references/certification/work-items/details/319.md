## 📝 Descrição

In the CallDynamicRegistrationEndpoint, after the first failure in this log, it request a wrong "tls_client_auth_subject_dn". It must consider OIDC specification.
https://web.conformance.directory.openbankingbrasil.org.br/log-detail.html?log=pW57iPXUbS4rOy1

As is: "tls_client_auth_subject_dn":"jurisdictionCountryName=BR,businessCategory=Private Organization,UID=bf4fe585-a56f-4c68-82fe-eb1265261727,serialNumber=43142666000197,CN=tpp.sandbox.openbanking.org.br,OU=74e929d9-33b6-4d85-8ba7-c146c867a817,O=Open Banking Brasil,L=Sao Paulo,ST=SP,C=BR"

As it should be (example): tls_client_auth_subject_dn":"UID=97e90169-9543-4ec5-b450-524a2d4756b4,1.3.6.1.4.1.311.60.2.1.3=#1302554b,2.5.4.15=#130f427573696e65737320456e74697479,2.5.4.5=#130e3433313432363636303030313937,CN=https://www.domainone.com.br,OU=74e929d9-33b6-4d85-8ba7-c146c867a817,O=Open Banking Brasil,L=Sao Paulo,ST=SP,C=BR"

domain-subjectdn-attributes-mappings:

CN: commonName

L: localityName

ST: stateOrProvinceName

O: organizationName

OU: organizationalUnitName

C: countryName

STREET: streetAddress

DC: domainComponent

UID: userId

"1.3.6.1.4.1.311.60.2.1.3": jurisdictionCountryName

"2.5.4.15": businessCategory

"2.5.4.5": serialNumber
