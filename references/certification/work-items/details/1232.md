Hi,

Thanks for opening the ticket!

The **`code_challenge_method`** is a required field when PKCE is being used. As PKCE is only required for the new FAPI profile, and the Conformance Suit does not use the new profile, it also does not use PKCE and therefore does not send the mentioned Field. In this case the conformance suit is operating as expected.
