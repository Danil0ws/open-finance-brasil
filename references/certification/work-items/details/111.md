## 📝 Descrição

As per the PIX standards the transaction id (txid) can have up to 35 characters

In pacs.008 The txid field, in the case of Dynamic QR, must have a minimum of 26 characters and a maximum of 35 characters.

>title: Id da Transação

>pattern: [a-zA-Z0-9]{26,35}

Looking at the specifications this field has a max length of 25 characters and thus is not in line with the PIX documentation.

        transactionIdentification:
          type: string
          pattern: '^[a-zA-Z0-9][a-zA-Z0-9]{0,24}$'
          maxLength: 25

This must be corrected to allow the QRDN to be tested
