## 📝 Descrição

## 1\. What happened and what were you expecting?

As seen at https://web.conformance.directory.openbankingbrasil.org.br/log-detail.html?log=CLEDEIP6ZkuwdPU#, the field chargeIdentificator is set as required always ([validator link](https://gitlab.com/raidiam-conformance/open-finance/certification/-/blob/master/src/main/java/net/openid/conformance/openbanking_brasil/creditCard/v2n/CreditCardAccountsTransactionValidatorV2n.java)), where it should only be when payment type is A_PRAZO, as defined at the swagger:

![image.png](/uploads/2527ea060d555f1ae3423bda044d6b66/image.png)

Besides that, as seen at the  [test module](https://gitlab.com/raidiam-conformance/open-finance/certification/-/blob/master/src/main/java/net/openid/conformance/openbanking_brasil/testmodules/creditCardApi/testmodule/abstractmodule/AbstractCreditCardApiMaxPageSizePagingTestModule.java), the `"Prepare to Fetch page 2 of Credit Card Tansactions"`  is returning a WARNING for all conditions, where it should be a FAILURE.
