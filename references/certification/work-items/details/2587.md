#### Current Behavior:

---

We received a ticket titled **"Non-compliance in the submission of PCM Payment Initiation reports – 03/05/2026 to 03/07/2026"**.

One of the records was flagged as **paired_inconsistent**, although all fields were reported correctly on our side.

Upon further analysis, we identified that the **x-fapi-interaction-id** associated with the reported case was used in requests to two different endpoints:

* `/open-banking/payments/v4/consents/{consentId}`
* `/open-banking/payments/v4/pix/payments/{paymentId}`

The **x-fapi-interaction-id** in question is:\
**f7AeC70a-519B-4b10-B362-0c71088A0341**

We discovered that this value was generated during an execution of the FVP, where the ITP (FVP engine) reused the same **x-fapi-interaction-id** across multiple endpoints.

The **clientId** of the ITP involved is:\
**sTZ5ZgDn6-GV0DKry0x36**

**Note:** This clientId corresponds to a request executed through the FVP.

#### De acordo com a documentação :

O cabeçalho x-fapi-interaction-id é um cabeçalho obrigatório para o client e **deve ser repetido pelo server** em sua resposta conforme especificação do Open Finance Brasil. Esse cabeçalho identifica uma requisição (e sua devida resposta) em específico, e não deve ser utilizado para identificar sequências de chamadas (jornadas, paginação, etc). **Cada par requisição-resposta deverá conter seu próprio valor nesse cabeçalho**.

Hello @rogeriorocha083. Thank you for the additional details.

After reviewing the case, we identified that the same x-fapi-interaction-id was unintentionally reused by the FVP across different endpoints during the execution of specific test modules. In some scenarios, the tool reused a previous request’s x-fapi-interaction-id instead of generating a new one. _A fix is planned for production in the current week._

Considering the documented [reconciliation](https://openfinancebrasil.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/OF/pages/37912631/Processamento) logic, we understand that this reuse can indeed affect the pairing result, since the first reconciliation step looks for a matching report based on fapiInteractionId, and only afterwards validates other fields such as clientOrgId, serverOrgId, statusCode, httpMethod, and endpoint.

![image.png](/uploads/dee3b9c6746a3edae380c86a81477dba/image.png){width="900" height="145"}

Because of that, the issue is not simply the reuse of the same x-fapi-interaction-id in more than one request, but its reuse in a near-concurrent context, before the original request/response pair has already been matched during reconciliation. Depending on the order of report reception and the record selected during reconciliation, this may result in either PAIRED_INCONSISTENT or UNPAIRED.

That said, our understanding is that this should not be treated as your non-compliance, since the ambiguity could have been introduced by the FVP reusing the same correlation identifier across distinct near requests.

So, while we are addressing this issue, we are sharing the original FVP reports so you can verify whether any other fields were actually inconsistent.
