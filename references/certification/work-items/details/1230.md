Hi Cassio! We have just reviewed your question, and this is an expected behavior for the test.

This test module intends to test the errors sent on a case of an invalid cpf sent on proxy, and not on the logged user. IN that sense, the test uses indeed use the cpf provided at the config for the logged user, as expected, but sends an invalid proxy when executing the payment.

That's why the error is just expected at the POST Payment, and not at the redirect, which would be the case in case an invalid CPF is sent. We have just updated the test summary to better explain what is expected by the test.

We are happy to help with any additional needs!
