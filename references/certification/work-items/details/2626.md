## 📝 Descrição

#### Current Behavior:
---
In payments_api_consent-bulk-cancel_negative_timezone_test-module_v5 test, the field payment.purpose is marked as required. However, according to the specification, the correct field name is payment.porpuse.

Failure summary:
FAILURE PostPaymentsConsentOASValidatorV5: payment.purpose is required when payment.type is PIX

#### Expected Behavior:
---
The test module should not validate the presence of the field payment.purpose. Instead, it must validate the presence of the field payment.porpuse.
