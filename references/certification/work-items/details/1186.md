Hi,

Thanks for opening the ticket!

The scopes for consents, openid and payments are present on the redirect_uri as described on the specification, and can be found on the following extract:

**scope=consents%20openid%20payments**

While the "%20" is an encoded representation of a space character. It's used to represent a space in a URL because spaces are not allowed in URLs.

In this sense, the conformance suit is working as expected and there are not fixes to be made on it. 

Feel free to open another ticket if you get any other issue!
