## 📝 Descrição

## 1. What happened and what were you expecting?

The credit-cards_api_resources_test-module_v2-1 execution is interrupted after `ConditionSequenceRepeater` finds a `nullPointerException`, as seen below:

![imagem_2023-09-26_110619789](/uploads/c4d65b61cd44dfa1148df8f3fcfec4d3/imagem_2023-09-26_110619789.png)

The error occurs when the authorization server incorrectly sends a 400 to a valid `GET /accounts` call at the Credit-cards-accounts API, in step _"Setting page-size=1000 and calling the credit card endpoint again"_.
