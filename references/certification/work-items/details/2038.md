## 📝 Descrição

When in the "consent" we have: schedule":{"monthly":{"startDate":"2025-02-13","dayOfMonth":31,"quantity":5}}

the payments are being scheduled for:

1. 03/01/2025.
2. 05/01/2025.
3. 07/01/2025.
4. 07/31/2025.

Notice that the second payment occurs almost 60 days later, which is incorrect. The correct schedule should be:

1. 03/01/2025.
2. 03/31/2025.
3. 05/01/2025.
4. 05/31/2025.
5. 07/01/2025.

Hello,

Thanks for reaching out.

Your consent request defines a monthly schedule with **dayOfMonth**: 31, quantity: 5, and startDate: "2025-02-13".
The expected behavior is that, for months without the 31st, the payment should fall on the 1st of the following month.
The Conformance Suite follows this rule correctly and schedules the payments accordingly in the POST /payments request.

 **Specification Reference:** 2.2.2.6 Divergência entre pagamento e consentimento: Valida se dados do pagamento são diferentes dos dados do consentimento (PAGAMENTO_DIVERGENTE_CONSENTIMENTO)
The consent does not predefine exact payment dates; it only specifies that payments should occur monthly, on the 31st.

Payment Schedule (POST /payments Request Sent by the Test Suite):

E44471172 **20250331** 1500OhY5LXwrrdY

E44471172 **20250501** 1500P4CKVy0ln1w

E44471172 **20250531** 1500MgKqghzsjNG

E44471172 **20250701** 1500DpoFHMAIy04


The dates match the expected pattern:

✅ March 1st (next day after February 31st does not exist)

✅ March 31st

✅ May 1st

✅ May 31st

✅ July 1st

While the Conformance Suit expected a 201 response code, the server returned "PAGAMENTO_DIVERGENTE_CONSENTIMENTO" in response. Taking into consideration that the correct dates are being sent on request, this seems to point an issue on the server side.

The Conformance Suite is scheduling payments in accordance with the specification.
