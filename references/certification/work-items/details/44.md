## 📝 Descrição

Hi all,

Tests from the payments suite are failing whenever optional fields (like `businessEntity` in a consent GET response) are present but with a `null` value. According to the [OBK development documentation](https://openbanking-brasil.github.io/areadesenvolvedor/#convencoes-de-payload), these cases should be treated equally (that is, a non-present field is equivalent to a field present with `null` value).
