## 1. What happened and what were you expecting?
The CreditCardAccountsTransactionValidatorV2n is incorrectly requiring the object `paymentType` as mandatory, however the cited field should only be mandatory when `transactionType` is `PAGAMENTO`. Below, it follows the error and the expected behaviour, mentioned in the swagger:
