## 📝 Descrição

As is common knowledge, recurring consents needs the field ibgeTownCode on the debtorAccount when the consent reaches AUTHORISED state, but the test does not provide an form field to input this value:

![image](/uploads/14b49e193d8d6834bfb84b33ec75f432/image.png){width=229 height=237}

even adding manually does not work:

![image](/uploads/d9a4e75da9a853b70db979bad5b1a47a/image.png){width=444 height=243}

Looking at the docs, it's not expected to have ibgeTownCode in debtorAccount when creating the enrollment and creating the recurring consent, so how should we retrive this field when authorizing an recurring consent in fido_flow?
