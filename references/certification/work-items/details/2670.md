## 📝 Descrição

**OCS-1059 - fvp-optimised-journey_enrollments-balances_test-module-v1**

The initial GET /accounts and GET /accounts/{id}/balances calls were being executed right after the authorization code (during PAR/redirect), out of order. 

Fix: the initial accounts + balance fetch + ExtractAvailableAmount were moved to after the enrollment reached AUTHORISED and the GET /consents AUTHORISED check, before the payment POST /consents.

**OCS-1057** **-** **fvp-optimised-journey_enrollments-invalid-request_test-module-v1** and **fvp-optimised-journey_enrollments-invalid_par_test-module-v1**

These tests were running `EnsureDailyLimitSetTo1BRLIfPresent` and `EnsureTransactionLimitSetTo1BRLIfPresent`, but these validations were not declared in the summaries (which cover only the rejection flow). Fix: both calls were removed from the two modules.
