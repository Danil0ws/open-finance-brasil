Hi Rodrigo! Thanks for the clarification!

The need for the x-fapi-interaction-id is currently defined at [BCLOG-F03-V2-003](https://openfinancebrasil.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/OF/pages/31162369/v2.0.0+Problemas+conhecidos).

The orientation until the publication for the ajust is:

- Informar cabeçalho x-fapi-interaction-id obrigatoriamente na requisição

In that sense, since its a mandatory item, an error should be returned when it is not sent.
