Thanks for contacting us!

The behavior of this test module has been validated in collaboration with the work group through the following issue:
https://gitlab.com/raidiam-conformance/open-finance/certification/-/issues/2030

In this test, a recurring-consent is created using randomly generated ISPB and creditor account information to ensure that the account does not belong to the account holder. The same random information is then used in the POST recurring-consents request. At this stage, no validation of account ownership is expected from the server, as this verification only occurs at the time of payment execution.

When the payment is processed, the SPI is called, and the transaction fails due to the non-existent account and ISPB being used. This results in the expected error 422 - TITULARIDADE_INCONSISTENTE.

Therefore, the test module is functioning as expected, and this behavior has been reviewed and confirmed with the work group.

Feel free to reach out if further clarification or assistance is needed!
