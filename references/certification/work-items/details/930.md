## 1\. What happened and what were you expecting?

After analysis, we believe there is an inconsistency, below are our guidelines: 

> Analysis by our Engineering team, it was validated that in item 1.3.2.3 of the documentation: https://openfinancebrasil.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/OF/pages/142672139/Informa+es+T+cnicas+-+Pagamentos+- +v3.0.0-beta.2
>
> We have the following guidance: 
>
> Payment details:
>
> Validates whether a specified parameter complies with business rules (DETALHE_PAGAMENTO_INVALIDO); 

We understand that because it is an optional field, we are validating whether the field complies with business rules and is within the payment details object. 

Within this scenario, we believe that the use of the DETALHE_PAGAMENTO_INVALIDO error code is much more appropriate. The parameter not informed is indicated as follows: 

Sending mandatory fields: Validates if all mandatory fields are informed (PARAMETRO_NAO_INFORMADO); 

> Therefore, we have a conditional and non-mandatory field and a payment detail. Therefore, we suggest opening an issue with Raidiam pointing out this situation.
