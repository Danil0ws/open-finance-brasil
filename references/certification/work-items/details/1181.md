Hi,

After thoroughly evaluating the test module related to the rejection field validation issue you reported, we have identified that the problem lies in the specification itself.

As described on the following specification, the presence of the rejection field is mandatory “if the consent status is REJECTED or REVOKED”:
https://openfinancebrasil.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/OF/pages/239370525/Informa+es+T+cnicas+-+SV+Pagamentos+Autom+ticos+-+v1.0.0-beta.5

As it is characterizes as an error on the spec we will be notifying the structure so it is fixed for when a newer version comes out. We will promptly fix it on the conformance suit once we have reached a understanding on this regard.

Feel free to open another ticket if you get any other issue!

Happy to help!
