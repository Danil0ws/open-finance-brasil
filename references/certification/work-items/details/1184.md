Hi Marcelino! Thanks for the ticket!

Regarding the fields that should be validated, the mains changes are:

1. The amount defined on the consent, whiten the totalAllowedAmount, is actually the total sum of payments that can be executed until the consent is consumed. In that sense, since the value is not defined on the consent, it can be different on the payment itself.
2. The creditor at the consent is now a list with the same owner for seeping accounts. As long the creditor is registered at the consent, it can have a payment executed against them.

All the other fields should be the same.

We are happy to help with any additional needs!
