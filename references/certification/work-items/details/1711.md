## 📝 Descrição

#### Current Behavior:

---

We found what seems to be an inconsistency between the following specifications:

- [Swagger de payments v4](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/OpenBanking-Brasil/openapi/main/swagger-apis/payments/4.0.0.yml) 
- [spec Idempotence - v4.0.0 - \[SV\] Payments](https://openfinancebrasil.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/OF/pages/347078709/Idempot+ncia+-+v4.0.0+-+SV+Payments)

While in Payments v4 Swagger idempotency errors are characterized by 422 ERROR_IDEMPOTENCY, in the idempotency spec they are treated with 422 NOT_INFORMED. 

Image from the Swagger:

![WhatsApp Image 2024-07-08 at 14.36.22 (2).jpeg](/uploads/e9c643cad3db0571192eb7c0ae04cf0e/WhatsApp_Image_2024-07-08_at_14.36.22__2_.jpeg)Image from the Payments v4 idempotency spec:

![WhatsApp Image 2024-07-08 at 14.36.22 (3).jpeg](/uploads/49a5cf3054d75f0bb829492e8a57f2a8/WhatsApp_Image_2024-07-08_at_14.36.22__3_.jpeg)The same behavior also occurs to Automatic Payments V1 specs.

* [API Automatic Payments swagger](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/OpenBanking-Brasil/openapi/main/swagger-apis/automatic-payments/1.0.0.yml)
* [Idempotência - v1.0.0 - \[SV\] Pagamentos Automáticos](https://openfinancebrasil.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/OF/pages/345178167/Idempot+ncia+-+v1.0.0+-+SV+Pagamentos+Autom+ticos)

Currently Conformance suit evaluates Idempotency erros based on Swagger definition, expecting ERROR_IDEMPOTENCY message.

What needs evaluation from GT is:

* What is the correct error message to be expected?
