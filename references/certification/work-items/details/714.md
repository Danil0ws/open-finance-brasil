## 📝 Descrição

## 1. What Happened and what were you expecting?

A few tests at `payments_test-plan_v2` do not send the mandatory header `x-fapi-interaction-id` in payments requests (common incorrect step: `FAPIBrazilCallPaymentConsentEndpointWithBearerToken`). Below it follows an example: 

![image](/uploads/253b8b5d7cb2c696d44fa66b808bbfb4/image.png)

The cited field is expected to be mandatory on the header request, according to [Informa #310](https://mailchi.mp/780543062934/open-banking-informa-9291225?e=%5BUNIQID%5D).
