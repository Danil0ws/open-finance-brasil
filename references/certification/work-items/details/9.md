## 📝 Descrição

Please use the following order when developing and releasing APIs:

1_Consentimento_Resources_e_Dados_Cadastrais

2_Contas 

3_Operações de Credito (All 4) e Cartão de Credito

Worth noting that the central bank has mandated a phased certification on those APIs. So you should expect 3 phased certification requests for functional apis, with 2 weeks space between them.

This shouldn't stop the full release as the FIs might wish to certify all APIs in one go.
