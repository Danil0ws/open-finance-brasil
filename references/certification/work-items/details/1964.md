## 📝 Descrição

#### Current Behavior:
---
In `automatic-payments_api_automatic-pix-semanal-core_test-module_v2` a payment attempt uses a CNPJ value in the field `data.document.identification` that is divergent from the CNPJ specified in the consent field `data.creditors.cpfCnpj`.

- CNPJ sent in POST recurring-consents (`92252126000171`):

  ![image](/uploads/a22081b7ba73337d29dc1281f498efde/image.png){width=380}

 
- CNPJ sent in POST recurring-payments (`65524414000165`):

  ![image](/uploads/440819811c7a97bc1e36946823de42fd/image.png){width=400}

This mismatch causes the implementation to respond with `PAGAMENTO_DIVERGENTE_CONSENTIMENTO` since the CNPJ in the payment document does not match the CNPJ in the consent creditors, leading the test to fail.

#### Expected Behavior:
---

According to the documentation ([API Automatic Payments v2.0.0-beta.2](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/OpenBanking-Brasil/openapi/main/swagger-apis/automatic-payments/2.0.0-beta.2.yml)), the field `data.document` in the POST recurring-payments request must be equal to one of the values sent in the consent creation stage (in the field `data.creditors`):
![image](/uploads/372c5a9a3adcde16b3c336603793578d/image.png)
