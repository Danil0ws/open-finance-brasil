## 1. From SD 11924

> The test engine is returning the following error:
> 
> FAILURE
> BusinessIdentificationValidatorV2
> Unable to find element civilName on the Business Identification V2 API response
> 
> 
> And it indicates the following snippet from the API response as the source of the problem:
> 
>     {
>       "personType": "PESSOA_LEGAL",
>       "type": "PARTNER",
>       "companyName": "STAR PARTICIPACOES LTDA",
>       "tradeName": "",
>       "shareholding": "0.500000",
>       "documentType": "CNPJ",
>       "documentNumber": "50895293",
>       "documentCountry": "BRA",
>       "documentIssueDate": "2021-09-10"
>     }
> 
> 
> As per the OAS specification published at https://openbanking-brasil.github.io/openapi/swagger-apis/customers/2.0.1.yml, the civilName field is not mandatory (line 1470) and is described (line 1497) with being the "Full civil name of the natural person". That is, it would be a field used for natural people only. This was not the case for the data above, indicated as the source of the error.
> 
> Another point to note, which may have influenced the mistaken implementation of the BusinessIdentificationValidatorV2 validation of requiring the field even for legal entities, is what is written in line 1499 of the OAS file. To know:
> 
> "[Restriction] The civilName field must be filled in when personType is PESSOA_JURIDICA."
> 
> We understand that the correct wording of this line should be:
> 
> "[Restriction] The civilName field must be filled in when personType is PESSOA_NATURAL."
> 
> 
> We suggest:
> 
> 1) Test correction, to not require civilName property for legal entities.
> 2) Add a known issue or correct the wording of the civilName property constraint, indicating that it is required only for the PERSON_NATURAL personType.
