## 📝 Descrição

## 1\. What happened and what were you expecting?

When running the test `automatic-payments_api_webhook-acsc_test-module_v1`, the following error appears:

![image](/uploads/78cd702862ee6238c5cc4ff90f766a40/image.png)

The consent webhook was sent twice, but for different statuses, namely:

1. When consent was `AUTHORISED`
2. When consent was `CONSUMED`

According to the specification, his scenario should accept either 1 or 2 consent webhook notifications:

![image](/uploads/b3ad8755d689f30a673731a9481d79c9/image.png)

This behavior is happening at:

* automatic-payments_api_webhook-acsc_test-module_v1
* automatic-payments_api_webhook-revoked_test-module_v1
