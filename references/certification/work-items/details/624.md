## 📝 Descrição

## 1. What Happened and what were you expecting?
This test creates a consent with localInstrument that is different from INIC, and sends the transactionIdentification field. In our test the payment request has the localInstrument "QRES", and there is no specification impling that we should not accept it.

As stated in the official documentation:
If localInstrument equals INIC, the transactionIdentification field must be filled in. If localInstrument equals MANU or DICT, the transactionIdentification field must not be filled. The account holder must validate whether the conditionality of the field has been met by the payment initiator.

https://openfinancebrasil.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/OF/pages/17376179/Informa+es+T+cnicas+-+Pagamentos+-+v1.2.0

What should we do? reject in case of QRES and QRDN also?
