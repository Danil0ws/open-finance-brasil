Hi Gutemberg! Thanks for the ticket!

The FVP executes a DCR everytime a new test plan is created, given that it does not store credentials.

Given this, all the possible erros at DCR can happen. Although having one client stored, making necessary the deletion of the cleint, the error can happen for sa lot of different reason.

In this case, as the image show, your server is returning the message "invalid or missing certificate"., although the certiricates used at the FVP are valid ones.

In that sense, to proceed with the test, you should ensure a sucessful DCR is executed. Please, feel free to reopne the ticket if have any additional evidence the error is coming from the FVP.

We are happy to help with any additional needs!
