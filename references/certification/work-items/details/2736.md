Hi, @zhidong.tan 

The Mock Bank validates the `cnpjInitiator` field against the Open Finance Brazil sandbox directory endpoint\
(`https://data.sandbox.directory.openbankingbrasil.org.br/roles`). It checks whether the provided CNPJ is registered as a `RegistrationNumber` of an organisation in the directory.

The CNPJ `50685362006773` you are sending **does not exist** as a registered organisation in either the sandbox or production directories:

- https://data.sandbox.directory.openbankingbrasil.org.br/participants
- https://data.directory.openbankingbrasil.org.br/participants

This is why Mock Bank returns `NAO_INFORMADO` / "CNPJ não registrado".

Please use your own organisation's CNPJ as listed in the directory (i.e., the CNPJ of the payment initiator - ITP - that is registered in the\
Open Finance ecosystem). Or you can use this one as well 44471172000119

### Answering your questions

1. **Why does Mock Bank return `NAO_INFORMADO`?** — Because the CNPJ `50685362006773` is not registered in the sandbox directory's `/roles `endpoint.
2. **Does Mock Bank expect `cnpjInitiator` to be pre-registered?** — Yes. It must be a valid CNPJ of an organisation registered in the Open Finance sandbox directory.
3. **Can you provide a valid CNPJ for testing?** — Any valid CNPJ that exists in the sandbox directory will work. Please use your own\
   organisation's registered CNPJ, or this one 44471172000119.
4. **Is `cnpjInitiator` required?** — Yes, for PIX payment initiation it is required, and it must match a registered organisation in the\
   directory.
