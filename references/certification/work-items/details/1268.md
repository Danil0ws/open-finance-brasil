## 📝 Descrição

## 1\. What happened and what were you expecting?

When the test module executes the step:

> Call the POST Consents with either the customer business or the customer personal permissions group, depending on whether the brazilCnpj has or not been provided.

When both both brazilCpf and brazilCnpj have been provided, both loggedUsed (which is a mandatory field) and businessEntity (which is optional) are sent on the request. While CUSTOMERS_PERSONAL permission group is not being sent, CUSTOMERS_BUSINESS permission group is not resulting the test to fail.

When a brazilCnpj is provided the permission groups sent on request are:

\["CUSTOMERS_PERSONAL_IDENTIFICATIONS_READ","CUSTOMERS_PERSONAL_IDENTIFICATIONS_READ","RESOURCES_READ"\]
