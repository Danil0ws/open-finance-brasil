The organizationIdentifier field is composed of the UID from the organisation not the UID from the software so they should not match.

When you call the PUT you should validate the following rule:

13. O valor do campo UID do certificado deve coincidir com o enviado no SSA, onde o campo UID deve conter o valor do campo software_id do SSA.
