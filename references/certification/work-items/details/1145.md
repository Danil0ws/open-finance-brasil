## 📝 Descrição



## 1. What happened and what were you expecting?

When running any of the `recurring-payments-*-core-test-module` (ie. the success tests) test modules, when the test module gets any of the payments to verify that they are scheduled, it also checks their endToEndId and expects the payments returned in the POST response to be in the same order as the payments in the request. This is affecting all success test modules for recurring payments plus the `payments_api_recurring-payments-patch_test-module_v4`


Nowhere in the specification is it stated that this is a requirement and is not a reasonable expectation.
