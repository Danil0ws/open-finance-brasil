## 📝 Descrição

#### Current Behavior:
---

Even after deducting R$ 1.00 from the "availableAmount.amount" field, the pooling continues until the test fails, reporting an error that it was waiting for the account value to be changed.

#### Expected Behavior:
---
The test performs a pooling operation expecting that the "availableAmount.amount" field of the account will have deducted the value of R$ 1.00 after the payment was created.
