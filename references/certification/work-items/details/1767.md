#### Current Behavior:
---
The test in the GET enrollment by id endpoint is expecting the response to have an `expirationDateTime`.

#### Expected Behavior:
---
We know that in the [swagger](https://openfinancebrasil.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/OF/pages/405995741/Informa+es+T+cnicas+-+SV+V+nculo+de+dispositivo+-+v1.3.1) this attribute is mandatory in the GET by id response, but in the [FAQ](https://openfinancebrasil.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/OF/pages/527925268/FAQ+-+JSR+-+v1.3.1+-+SV+V+nculo+de+dispositivo) says that the `expirationDateTime` will be informed by the user at the moment of the authorization in the holder institution
![Captura_de_Tela_2024-08-15_às_11.43.21](/uploads/da276f1dd4dc767fa7026f8df4431e6c/Captura_de_Tela_2024-08-15_às_11.43.21.png)

In this case we have a conflict of definitions, according to the FAQ this information will be available at the moment of the authorization (status `AWAITING_ENROLLMENT`), and the test and the swagger says that this information is mandatory independent of the current status of the enrollment, what is the correct behavior? Looks like this attribute should allow nullable values
