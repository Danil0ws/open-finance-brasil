## 📝 Descrição

Hello!

Mock bank doesn't recognize certificate issued with new PKI from Open Finance sandbox directory.

As reported #406 (https://mailchi.mp/481be4feaf42/open-banking-informa-9378404?e=2eb7798bd9), since 06/19/2023, new certificates issued will have a new chain ("Open Finance Sandbox Issuing CA - G2").

Mock Bank APIs are rejecting mtls connection due to not recognizing issuer of new certificate.

The root and issuer CA are available at:

http://crl.pki-g2.sandbox.directory.openbankingbrasil.org.br/root-ca.pem
http://crl.pki-g2.sandbox.directory.openbankingbrasil.org.br/issuer-ca.pem

Best regards,

George Silva (Banco do Nordeste).
