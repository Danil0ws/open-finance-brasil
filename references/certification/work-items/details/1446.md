## 1. What happened and what were you expecting?

**What happened?**

During our system checks, we've identified two issues with the certificate chains at certain Admin API v2 endpoints:

a) In the case of [this participant's endpoint ](https://openbanking.alfa.com.br/open-banking/admin/v2/metrics ), there's an inconsistency where the Issuer's name on the leaf certificate does not match the Subject's name on the intermediary certificate (GeoTrust EV RSA CA G2) <> (GeoTrust EV RSA CA 2018). This mismatch breaks the trust chain and prevents our system from establishing a secure connection.

b) For this [other participant's endpoint](https://openbanking.socinal.com.br/open-banking/admin/v2/metrics), the certificate chain has an improper structure where both the leaf and the intermediary certificates have the same Subject and Issuer (participant as Subject and CA as Issuer), resulting in a circular reference which invalidates the certificate chain.



**What were you expecting?**

We expect the conformance suite to be complemented with validation tests that can:

a) Verify that the Issuer's name on the leaf certificate correctly corresponds to the Subject's name on the intermediary certificate. This check ensures a valid issuer-subject chain, establishing trust within our system.

b) Validate the hierarchical structure of the certificate chain, ensuring there are no role reversals or circular references. Each certificate in the chain should be properly positioned to certify the entity below it, culminating with the participant’s certificate
 
This behaviour is in line with what's seen on the test fvp-payments-consents-server-certificate-v2 which validates the trust chain that's sent, however, here there's no requirement to verify that the Certificate Issuer is authorized
