## 📝 Descrição

## 1. What happened and what were you expecting?

After POST enrollments, they are validated in the **AWAITING_RISK_SIGNALS** status. At the end of the 5 minutes for expiration, the engine expects the enrollment to be rejected for the reason **REJECTED_TEMPO_EXPIRADO_AUTHORISATION**, but in this scenario the expected reason should be **REJECTED_TEMPO_EXPIRADO_RISK_SIGNALS** according to the technical specification.

Logs: https://web.conformance.directory.openbankingbrasil.org.br/log-detail.html?log=N79ZnFaE0FT83pD

Hi,

Thanks for opening the ticket!

The failure occurred when enrollment was rejected due to expiry while in status AWAITING_RISK_SIGNALS,

This seems to characterize a discrepancy between what is available at the API specification page and the swagger itself.

1. As can be seen from[ API Enrollments for Payment Initiation specification page](https://openfinancebrasil.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/OF/pages/297501394/Informa+es+T+cnicas+-+SV+V+nculo+de+dispositivo+-+v1.3.0), rejection reasons "REJEITADO_TEMPO_EXPIRADO_RISK_SIGNALS" appears as a valid option to be returned:

   ![image.png](/uploads/9b95d6922b942d8b8ac98e8fd95cd36a/image.png){width="305" height="85"}
2. Although when looking at Enum for the field, the expected rejection reasons are:

   ![image.png](/uploads/48e4939a8584f754a61a09dcb026abd7/image.png){width="296" height="126"}
3. Adding to that, what is available at the [API Swagger](https://openbanking-brasil.github.io/openapi/swagger-apis/enrollments/1.3.0.yml) is:

   ![image.png](/uploads/834a5abfcf9bc2305b6eeb02b98ec8a7/image.png){width="380" height="115"}

Therefore, what needs to be evaluated by the WG:

1. Is the rejection reasons "REJEITADO_TEMPO_EXPIRADO_RISK_SIGNALS" a valid one?
2. Is the rejection reason "REJEITADO_TEMPO_EXPIRADO_AUTHORISATION" no longer valid?
3. Which one should be considered in this scenario?

This discrepancy also seems to be present on v1.3.0 specification.
