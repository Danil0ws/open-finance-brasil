# Overview

Customer Data is what a customer authorises a receiving institution to read:
their registration data, their accounts and cards, their credit operations, and
their investments. It spans two phases of the Open Finance Brasil programme, and
the suite groups them here because a participant certifies them through the same
consent flow.

Phase 2 carries the consent and resource machinery plus the account and credit
APIs. Phase 4B carries the investment APIs. They release on their own schedules,
so each has its own page.
