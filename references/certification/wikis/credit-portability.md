# Overview

Credit portability lets a customer move an existing credit contract from one
institution to another. The proposing institution reads the contract through the
Credit Operations APIs, sends a portability request, and the two sides settle it.

The suite covers two products, and they are separate because the contracts they
act on are different: unsecured personal credit, and federal payroll credit. Each
has its own eligibility rules, its own status flow and its own data requirements,
so each has its own page and its own certification cut-off.
